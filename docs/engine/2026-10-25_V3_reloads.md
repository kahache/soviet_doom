# V3 reloads: magazines with no new art (experiment, 2026-10-25)

Owner: `mod-engineer`. Branch `v3-reload` only. D132 asked to "explore
reloads" if Gemini's quota allowed reload frames; it does not until
tomorrow, so this is the version **without new art**, an option the CEO
plays with and without and then keeps or drops (a nice to have, D121; the
Icebox entry "Weapon reloads"). The decision record is ADR 014; the
numbers are `BALANCE.md` §10; the contract is `MOD-SPEC.md` §6.15.
**Nothing here has run in the engine (D26).**

## 1. What the player gets

- **Four guns have a magazine:** TT-33 8 rounds, PPSh-41 the 71-round
  drum, AKM 30, ROKS-3 an 80-jet fuel tank. The ice axe, the RPG-7, the
  hand Katyusha and the RGD-5 have none (the RPG-7 and the Katyusha already
  reload from the muzzle in their Fire frames).
- **The IZh-58** keeps its two barrels and its break-open after the second
  shot; with reloads on, the reload key also breaks it open after one.
- **Automatic reload** when the magazine runs dry: the gun goes down and up
  at once (if the trigger is held, the next shot comes after the reload).
  Not when the player has just asked for another weapon: the switch wins.
- **The reload key, R by default** (`KEYCONF`: `defaultbind r +reload`,
  which binds R only if neither R nor `+reload` is bound already). The
  player can move it in Options > Customize Controls > "Reload" (the
  engine's own row). From the console: `bind r +reload`. The key does
  nothing with a full magazine or an empty pool.
- **The animation** has no frames of its own: the gun is lowered off screen
  and raised again, as on a weapon switch, with the gun's existing reload
  sound while it is out of sight (`SDTTRLD`, `SDPPRLD`, `SDRKRLD`, recorded
  for this in D74 and parked by D47; the AKM reuses `SDPPRLD` until it has a
  take). The magazine is full again the tic the gun starts back up.
- **A readout:** "rounds/capacity" in small gold digits for the gun in
  hand, just above the status bar's AMMO number (or above the fullscreen
  HUD's ammo), red when the magazine is empty. The status bar's big AMMO
  number is still the whole pool.
- **A switch:** Options > Soviet Doom > **Weapon reloads** (On/Off), the
  CVar `sd_reloads`, on by default on this branch, saved in the player's
  ini. Off, the game is exactly v1.2's; it can be flipped at any moment.

| Gun | Magazine | Reload | Sound starts | Down / out / up (tics) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TT-33 | 8 | 42 tics, 1.2 s | tic 8 (the slide slams at tic 32, as the gun rises) | 10 / 22 / 10 |
| PPSh-41 | 71 | 88 tics, 2.5 s | tic 42 (the bolt caught at tic 72) | 16 / 56 / 16 |
| AKM | 30 | 70 tics, 2.0 s | tic 26 (the bolt at tic 56) | 14 / 42 / 14 |
| ROKS-3 | 80 | 88 tics, 2.5 s | tic 41 (the igniter armed at tic 72) | 16 / 56 / 16 |
| IZh-58 | 2 | 30 tics, its own frames, as before | as before | — |

## 2. How it works (for the next engineer)

- **A magazine is a counter over the pool**, not a second pool. Firing takes
  the round from `Clip`, `SD_AkmAmmo` or `Cell` as before; the weapon's
  `sdMagFired` counts the rounds since the last reload and is raised in
  an override of `Weapon.DepleteAmmo`, which every shot of these guns goes
  through, so **no Fire frame changed** for the counting. No ammo is
  created, lost or moved: the maps' ammunition (`BALANCE.md` §8) stands.
  Rounds loaded = capacity minus rounds fired, never more than the pool
  (the TT-33 and the PPSh-41 share `Clip`, so the other gun may have spent
  them). A reload loads what the pool holds, up to the capacity.
- **The code is one mixin**, `SD_MagazineWeapon` in
  `zscript/weapons/magazine.zs`, included by the four guns (they share no
  base class: Pistol, Chaingun, DoomWeapon, PlasmaRifle). Its two fields
  are ordinary fields, so **they go into the saved game**; a save from
  before this branch loads with full magazines. `SD_Reloads` holds the
  CVar test, the animation curve and the HUD's lookup.
- **The states**, per gun: a 0-tic guard as the first Fire frame (and
  before the PPSh-41's second round) that jumps to `Reload` when the
  magazine is dry; `A_SD_WeaponReady` (still the one Ready frame) jumps to
  `Reload` on a dry magazine and passes `WRF_ALLOWRELOAD` to
  `A_WeaponReady` when a reload would load; and the `Reload` / `ReloadLoop`
  states: one 1-tic frame repeated for the reload's length, each tic
  setting the sprite's height with `A_WeaponOffset(0, y, WOF_INTERPOLATE)`
  (y from 32, the rest position, to 128, off screen, and back), the sound
  on its tic and the refill when the gun starts up. The guards only jump
  with `sd_reloads` on, so off, every tic of every gun is v1.2's.
- **The engine's own reload button.** `+reload` sets `BT_RELOAD`; when the
  weapon's last `A_WeaponReady` had `WRF_ALLOWRELOAD` (16, gzdoom.pk3
  `constants.zs`), the engine jumps to the weapon's `Reload` state. The
  IZh-58 already had one; it is now reachable that way too, with reloads on.
- **The readout** is an `EventHandler`, `SD_MagazineHud`
  (`zscript/sd/magazine_hud.zs`, last in MAPINFO's `AddEventHandlers`), so
  `SD_StatusBar` is untouched; removing it is one MAPINFO name.

## 4. What this does to balance

`BALANCE.md` §10 has the table. In one line: sustained DPS falls 25-37 %
(TT-33 26.3 → 19.8, PPSh-41 93.3 → 66.0, AKM 105 → 66.3, ROKS-3 157.5 →
115.2; the IZh-58 stays 73.7), which **puts slot 4 under slot 3** for long
fights; a full magazine's burst is unchanged. Shorter reloads are the knob
if the CEO wants slot 4 back on top (the table in §10). The maps'
ammunition is not touched.

## 5. Not done, and why

- **No reload frames** (D132: no Gemini quota). When frames exist, they
  replace the lowered `A` frame inside `ReloadLoop`; the timings and the
  sound tics stay.
- **No AKM reload take:** `sd/weapon/akm/reload` plays the PPSh-41's
  `SDPPRLD` (SNDINFO, "REUSED -> SDAKRLD"), like the AKM's other sounds.
- **No `docs/ru/` mirror** (D34: at phase close).
- **The Icebox entry, the Weapon-sounds doc §6 and the PRD** are the PM's
  and `sound-designer`'s to update if the CEO keeps the mechanic.
