# The RGD-5 hand grenade: slot 8 (V3, A4)

Owner: `mod-engineer`. Status: **built for V3 in a worktree, on provisional
sprites (D116), not in v1.2, not seen in the engine (D26)**; placed in
MAP04-MAP09 and the range on 2026-10-24 as §6 advises (`BALANCE.md` §8.6).
Dated snapshot, 2026-10-23. Decision: D120 (the CEO chose proposal A4
for V3, `docs/ICEBOX.md` "V3 scope"; costs in
`docs/product/2026-10-23_P4_next-version-proposals.md`). The
engine decisions (slot, pool, fuse, the loose grenade) are **ADR 012**
(`docs/engine/adr/012-rgd5-hand-grenade.md`); this document is the design,
the balance, the art and sound brief, the placement advice and the checks.

## 1. What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| Weapon | **RGD-5**, the Soviet Army's standard fragmentation hand grenade since 1954: a 58 mm olive steel egg, 310 g, the UZRGM fuse with its ring and spoon |
| Classes | `SD_Rgd5 : DoomWeapon` (the hand, and the pouch of two on the floor), `SD_Rgd5Ammo : Ammo` (the pool and one loose grenade), `SD_Rgd5Box : SD_Rgd5Ammo` (a crate), `SD_Rgd5Grenade : Actor` (the thrown grenade, not placeable). None replaces anything |

| Slot | **8, a key of its own** (GZDoom binds 8 to `slot 8` by default; ADR 012 weighs slots 7, 5 and 1 and a quick-throw key). `SD_Player`: `Player.WeaponSlot 8, "SD_Rgd5"` |
| Ammo | **its own pool**, "RGD-5 grenades": the grenade is its own ammunition; a loose grenade also gives the weapon (§3) |
| Editor numbers | **21009** `SD_Rgd5` (the pouch, 2 grenades), **21021** `SD_Rgd5Ammo` (1 grenade), **21022** `SD_Rgd5Box` (a crate, 4). Checked free in `src/lumps/MAPINFO`, `tools/`, every map and every doc on 2026-10-23 |
| Strings | `SD_TAG_RGD5`, `SD_PICKUP_RGD5`, `SD_OB_RGD5`, `SD_AMMO_RGD5`, `SD_GOT_RGD5`, `SD_GOT_RGD5BOX`, in `[enu default]`, `[ru]` and `[ca]` |

In the fiction it needs no explaining: every Soviet soldier of the 1970s
carried two in a pouch on the belt.

## 2. Behaviour and balance

Every number is a named constant in the three files with its reason in one
line; this is the longer why. Ruler: `BALANCE.md` §0 (35 tics a second;
Doom's projectile damage `Damage × random(1,8)`, average ×4.5).

**The throw.** Press fire (one press, one throw: `+WEAPON.NOAUTOFIRE`, no
`A_ReFire`): the pin comes out and the arm goes back (8 tics), the arm comes
forward (3), the grenade leaves (2), the follow-through (8), the next grenade
comes to hand (12, `A_CheckReload`: with none left the engine switches to the
best gun). **33 tics, about one throw a second.** The grenade leaves along
the view with **10° of loft** (`SD_RGD5_LOFT`, negative because GZDoom's
pitch is positive downwards; `A_FireProjectile` adds it to the view's pitch
around `SpawnPlayerMissile`, gzdoom.pk3 g4.14.2 `stateprovider.zs`), autoaim
off (`FPF_NOAUTOAIM`: autoaim would flatten the arc onto a monster), at
**24 units a tic**, under Doom's gravity (1 unit a tic², `-NOGRAVITY`).
The loft is clamped so view plus loft never passes 80° up (a throw at the
ceiling never tips past the vertical and flies backwards).

| Aim | first floor contact on the flat (from ~32 units up) |
|---|---|
| level (10° up) | **~310 units** |
| 20° up (30° total) | ~550 units |
| 30° up (40° total) | ~600 units |

(`x = v cos θ · t`, `t` from `h + v sin θ · t − t²/2 = 0`; the spawn height
of a player's missile is the engine's, **NOT VERIFIED**; then a short
bounce adds a little.) Doom rooms are 256-512 units across: aimed level it
lands inside the next room; aimed up it reaches the far side of a hall.

**The bounce.** `BounceType "Doom"`, `+BOUNCEONACTORS` (Strife's
`HEGrenade`'s flags): it bounces off walls (×0.4 of its speed), floors and
ceilings (×0.35) and monsters, then slides to rest. Short on purpose: a
grenade that ricochets across the map cannot be aimed round a corner.

**The fuse: 60 tics, 1.71 s from the throw** (`SD_RGD5_FUSE_TICS`). The
real UZRGM burns 3.2-4.2 s; the brief asked for 1.5-2 s so the throw is not
a wait and a monster does not walk out of the blast (a trooper walks ~150
units in 1.7 s). Flight takes ~0.4 s aimed level, so it lies ~1.3 s on the
floor. It is our own counter, not Strife's (`ADR 012`, "The fuse"): `Death`
means only "stopped", and the burst is the `Detonate` state the fuse alone
reaches. A grenade still in the air when the fuse ends bursts in the air
(it is stopped and its gravity switched off first).

**The blast.**

| | PG-7V (RPG-7) | **RGD-5** | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| impact | 30 × random(1,8), avg **135** | **none** (`Damage 0`; it bounces off monsters) | a grenade is not a shell |
| blast at the centre | 100 | **100** | the mod's explosive scale; under the PG-7V's 235 on the one it hits |
| blast radius | 104 (shaped charge) | **128** (Doom's rocket's) | fragmentation: the group tool |
| falloff | linear, no core | linear, no core (`SD_RGD5_FULL_RADIUS 0`) | Doom's `A_Explode` |
| hurts the thrower | yes | **yes** (`XF_HURTSOURCE`) | the risk: a grenade back off a wall |
| wakes monsters | no | **yes** (`alert`, as Strife's) | it lands in the room before you do |
| bosses | impact only (`+NORADIUSDMG`) | **nothing** | a grenade never does a boss's work |
| a unit | ~235 on one target | **≤ 100 on each target** | 43 % of the PG-7V on one target |

"Under the RPG-7 by unit" holds on any single target (100 against ~235) and
on a tight group (four troopers 64 units from the burst: the grenade 4 × 50
= 200; a PG-7V on one of them 235 + 3 × 38 = 350). The grenade wins only
where the RPG-7 cannot reach: **a spread group** (at 96 units the grenade
still does 25, the PG-7V's blast 8) and **no line of sight** (round a
corner, over a barricade, down a stairwell). Over its whole disc the blast
carries 1.5 × the PG-7V's blast (`100 × 128² / 3` against `100 × 104² / 3`),
with no impact on top.

**Who dies** (Doom's linear falloff, `damage = 100 × (1 − d / 128)`, `d`
measured to the target's edge as Doom does; **NOT VERIFIED** in the engine;
hit points from `BESTIARY.md` §12):

| Enemy | HP | one grenade kills inside | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reich rifleman | 20 | **102 units** | most of the disc |
| Legion trooper | 30 | **90 units** | the Legion's squads are its target |
| Hybrid Stormtrooper | 60 | 51 units | wounds him beyond that |
| Legion gunner | 80 | 26 units | a direct drop at his feet |
| Reich hound | 150 | two grenades inside 32 | not a grenade's job |
| Doughboy, Panzermensch, heavy | 400, 500, 1000 | 4, 5, 10 at the centre | not a grenade's job |
| the General, the colossus, the Consortium | — | **never** (`+NORADIUSDMG`) | |

**Never raised by itself.** `+WEAPON.NO_AUTO_SWITCH` (picking it up does not
raise it), `+WEAPON.NOAUTOSWITCHTO` (nor does picking up a grenade with an
empty gun in hand) and `Weapon.SelectionOrder 4000` (after the ice axe's
3700: when every gun is dry the engine raises the axe, not a grenade).
`+WEAPON.EXPLOSIVE` marks it as a splash weapon for the engine.

## 3. Ammunition: the grenade is its own

**`SD_Rgd5Ammo : Ammo`, a pool of its own** (ADR 012; the mod's second
after the AKM's, ADR 011): a thrown weapon fires no cartridge, and sharing
`RocketAmmo` would let grenades eat PG-7V rounds.

| Constant | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `SD_RGD5_LOOSE` | 1 | one grenade on the floor (21021) |
| `SD_RGD5_AMMO_GIVE` (the weapon) | 2 | the pouch on the floor (21009) holds two, as the soldier's belt pouch |
| `SD_RGD5_CRATE` (`SD_Rgd5Box`) | 4 | a crate is a reward, not a supply line (the RPG-7's crate gives 5) |
| `SD_RGD5_MAX` | 6 | three pouches: scarce on purpose (D119: ammunition "always in surplus") |
| `SD_RGD5_BACKPACK` | **0** | a backpack hands out every pool (`BackpackItem.CreateCopy`); a grenade without the weapon to throw it would read as a bug |
| `SD_RGD5_BACKPACK_MAX` | 12 | Doom's rule: the backpack doubles the room |

**A loose grenade is the weapon too.** `SD_Rgd5Ammo.TryPickup` (the crate
inherits it) takes the ammunition by the engine's rules first (refused when
the pool is full); then, if the toucher owns no `SD_Rgd5`, it spawns one,
sets `AmmoGive1 = 0` and hands it over with `CallTryPickup`, destroying it
if refused. The line printed is the grenade's ("An RGD-5 grenade."), not the
weapon's; the weapon is not raised. **NOT VERIFIED** in the engine.

Skills 1 and 5 double ammo pickups and the weapon's 2, as for every pool.
No monster drops grenades: a recommendation for later is §6.

**The status bar.** As for the AKM: bar A's ammunition table lists Doom's
four pools, so the grenade count shows only in the big AMMO number while
the grenade is held, and in the fullscreen HUD with the pool's icon
(`SDHBA0` today). **NOT VERIFIED.** Slot 8 has no digit on the ARMS panel
(it shows 2-7). Debt, §8.

**Missing for a finished weapon:** the five finals above. Not asked for:
a dry click (with no grenade left the engine switches away) and a fuse
hiss while it lies there (it would need a looping sound and a channel;
nice to have, ~10k).

## 8. Known gaps and debt

- **Not seen in the engine (D26).** The fuse jump, the bounce turning into
  `Death`, the loose grenade giving the weapon, the loft and clamp, the sky
  (`+SKYEXPLODE`: a grenade thrown into a sky should stop and fall back,
  not vanish) and the `$pitchset` are all **NOT VERIFIED**.
- **In hand it looks like the ice axe** until Gemini's frames land; the
  pouch's `Scale` may or may not reach the first-person sprite (it should
  not: a weapon's held frames scale by `Weapon.WeaponScaleX/Y`, not by the
  actor's `Scale`; **NOT VERIFIED**).
- **No grenade count on bar A** and no digit for slot 8 on the ARMS panel
  (§3); the count shows in the AMMO number while it is held.
- **No cooking** (holding the grenade after the pin is pulled) and **no
  quick-throw key** (ADR 012, option 5): ideas for later.
- A thrown grenade can roll down a slope or off a ledge; with
  `MaxStepHeight 4` it does not climb steps. Whether it settles on a lift
  or a moving floor is untested.
