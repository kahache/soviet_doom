## 2. Balance

Every number is a named constant in `akm.zs` / `akm_ammo.zs` with the
reason in one line; this is the longer why. Ruler and conventions:
`docs/engine/BALANCE.md` §0-§2 (hitscan damage `base × random(1,3)`,
averages use ×2, 35 tics a second).

| | PPSh-41 (mod) | **AKM (draft)** | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage per round | 4 × random(1,3) = 4-12, avg **8** | **6** × random(1,3) = 6-18, avg **12** | +50 % (the TT-33's round) |
| Cycle | 3 tics, 700 rpm | **4 tics** (2 + 2), **525 rpm** | slower |
| Rounds/s | 11.67 | **8.75** | −25 % |
| DPS (all landing) | 93.3 | **105.0** | +12.5 % |
| Spread, round 1 | pinpoint | pinpoint | = |
| Spread, rounds 2-3 | ±7.0° | **±2.0°** | much tighter |
| Spread, round 4 on | ±7.0° | **±5.0°**, plus ±1.5° vertical (the muzzle climbs) | tighter |
| Ammo | `Clip` (7.62×25), shared with the TT-33 | `SD_AkmAmmo` (7.62×39), its own | §3 |
| Selection order | 700 | **650** | the engine prefers the AKM |

**Why these numbers.**

- **4 tics.** The real AKM cycles at about 600 rpm (3.5 tics). 3 tics is
  the PPSh's 700 rpm and would make the two the same gun with different
  dice; 4 tics (525 rpm) is the nearest honest value that keeps the AKM
  slower, as the brief asks. Same reasoning as BALANCE §3's PPSh.
- **Avg 12 per round.** The 7.62×39 is a rifle cartridge, twice the
  7.62×25's energy; +50 % per round against the PPSh, equal to the TT-33's
  (the pistol's round is the mod's "one aimed shot" unit).
- **105 DPS keeps the ladder.** BALANCE §2: 19 → 26 → 74 → 93 → 182 → 210 → 394.
  The AKM slots in at **105**, above the PPSh and well under the RPG-7's
  181.7. It is slot 4's better gun, as the SSG is Doom II's better slot 3,
  and it pays for it with scarcer ammunition (§3).
- **Accurate in short bursts.** `A_FireBullets` already makes a single
  bullet with `player.refire == 0` pinpoint (gzdoom.pk3 g4.14.2,
  `zscript/actors/inventory/stateprovider.zs`, read 2026-10-22). `A_ReFire`
  adds one to `player.refire` per round while the trigger is held and zeroes
  it on release (same file), so `refire` is "rounds already fired in this
  burst". `A_SD_FireAkm` passes ±2.0° for rounds 2-3 and ±5.0° / ±1.5°
  from round 4. Tap-tap-tap is a rifle; holding the trigger is a PPSh with
  a better round. VERIFIED as code; **NOT VERIFIED in the engine.**

**What the spread means at range.** The offset of each round is
`spread × Random2() / 255` (same file), Doom's triangular distribution on
[−spread, +spread]. A 20-radius infantryman, aimed at the centre:

| Distance | target's half-width | AKM rounds 2-3 (±2°) | AKM round 4+ (±5°) | PPSh any round after the 1st (±7°) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 512 | 2.24° | **100 %** | 69 % | 54 % |
| 1024 | 1.12° | **81 %** | 40 % | 29 % |

(horizontal only; `P = 1 − (1 − a/spread)²`, a ruler like BALANCE's DPS
column). A three-round burst at 1024 units puts ~2.6 of 3 rounds on target
against the PPSh's ~1.6: the AKM is the gun for the long sightlines of
Episodes 2 and 3 (the dam, Red Square, the airfield).

**Against the bestiary** (`ceil(HP / avg damage)`, every round landing;
HP from `BESTIARY.md` §12 and, for the two drafts, `2026-10-22_P4_ep2-enemies.md`):

| Enemy | HP | PPSh-41 rounds | **AKM rounds** | AKM magazines |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reich rifleman | 20 | 3 | **2** | 0.07 |
| Legion trooper | 30 | 4 | **3** | 0.10 |
| Hybrid Stormtrooper | 60 | 8 | **5** | 0.17 |
| Legion gunner (draft) | 80 | 10 | **7** | 0.23 |
| Reich hound | 150 | 19 | **13** | 0.43 |
| Doughboy | 400 | 50 | **34** | 1.1 |
| Panzermensch | 500 | 63 | **42** | 1.4 |
| Heavy Panzermensch | 1000 | 125 | **84** | 2.8 |
| The General | 3000 | 375 | **250** | 8.3 |
| Liberty Colossus (draft) | 4500 | 563 | **375** | 12.5 |

The AKM is an infantry gun: it clears riflemen, troopers and gunners
cheaply and carries a fight against one Doughboy or Panzermensch; the
bosses still need the RPG-7 and the Katyusha (the pool's 150-round cap is
half a General).

## 3. Ammunition: its own pool

**Decision: `SD_AkmAmmo`, a fifth pool, "7.62x39 rounds".** Reasons:

1. **The mod's own rule for sharing does not hold.** BALANCE §3/§4 share
   `Clip` between the TT-33 and the PPSh-41 *because* they fire the same
   7.62×25 Tokarev round. The AKM fires 7.62×39; sharing would contradict
   the reason the table gives.
2. **Sharing would retire the PPSh.** From one pool the AKM gives 12
   damage a round against 8 and more DPS (105 against 93.3); there would be
   no reason ever to raise the PPSh again. Two pools make them a choice:
   the PPSh is the abundant, wasteful gun (every rifleman's clip feeds it),
   the AKM the efficient, scarce one.
3. **The cost is small and known:** one `Ammo` class (also the magazine
   pickup, as Doom's `Clip` is both), one editor number (21020), one sprite
   (`SDAM`), two strings per language. `MOD-SPEC.md` §6.4 says "the mod
   creates no new ammo pools"; that sentence is amended at integration and
   the reason recorded in an ADR (§7).

| Constant | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `SD_AKM_MAG` (pickup) | 30 | one magazine |
| `SD_AKM_AMMO_GIVE` (the weapon) | 30 | the rifle comes with one magazine in it |
| `SD_AKM_MAX` | 150 | five magazines; `Clip` holds 200 of a pistol round |
| `SD_AKM_BACKPACK` | 30 | Doom's rule: the backpack gives one small pickup of every pool |
| `SD_AKM_BACKPACK_MAX` | 300 | Doom's rule: the backpack doubles the cap |

**Dropped magazines.** A monster's dropped ammo carries half its amount
unless the `DropItem` names one (`Ammo.ModifyDropAmount`, gzdoom.pk3
g4.14.2 `zscript/actors/inventory/ammo.zs`, read 2026-10-22: no amount →
`max(1, Amount × 0.5)` at the default skill factor; an explicit amount is
used as is). So `DropItem "SD_AkmAmmo"` gives **15 rounds**, and
`DropItem "SD_AkmAmmo", 256, 30` a full magazine. **Recommendation (the
brief):** the Legion's heavy gunner (`SD_LegionGunner`, draft, Episode 2)
drops `SD_AkmAmmo` (15) instead of the PPSh-41 he drops today; the
Legion's heavy infantry carries captured Warsaw Pact stocks. 15 rounds is
~180 damage, more than his own 80 HP: killing gunners with the AKM pays for
itself at about a 45 % hit rate, so the AKM lives on the gunners and the
PPSh on the riflemen. That line is in the ep2-enemies overlay, not this
one; it changes when either is integrated (§7).

**The status bar.** Bar A's ammo table on the right lists Doom's four
pools only; the AKM's rounds show in the big AMMO number while it is held
(DoomStatusBar draws the current weapon's ammo) and in the fullscreen HUD
with the `SDAM` icon. **NOT VERIFIED in the engine.** A fifth row on bar A
is an art change (`art-director`) and a `SD_StatusBar` change; debt, §8.

## 8. Known gaps and debt

- **The slot.** `SD_Player` lists `Player.WeaponSlot 4, "SD_Ppsh"` only. In
  the overlay the AKM reaches slot 4 through `Weapon.SlotNumber 4`:
  `Weapon.CheckAddToSlots` returns it for a class that replaces nothing
  (gzdoom.pk3 g4.14.2 `weapons.zs`), and the engine's C++ is expected to add
  such "extra" weapons to the player's slots. The C++ half is **NOT
  VERIFIED**; if key 4 never reaches the AKM in the engine, the overlay
  still works with `give SD_Akm` (the gun is raised on pickup) and the
  integration's step 3 fixes it for good. Which of the two comes first on
  key 4 is **NOT VERIFIED**.
- **Bar A** shows no AKM row (§3). Integration-time debt for
  `art-director` + `mod-engineer` (~25k), or accepted as is (the count shows
  in the main number while the gun is up).
- **The provisional tint is not palette-checked**: the overlay's PNGs are
  truecolour like every draft (MOD-SPEC §6.9).
- **The AKM is not in the practice range** (`RANGE`); add it at
  integration with the other weapons there.
