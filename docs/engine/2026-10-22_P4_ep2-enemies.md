**Editor numbers.** The brief proposed 21107 and 21108. Neither is free:
21107 is `SD_PanzermenschSchwer` (MAPINFO, MOD-SPEC §6.1) and 21108 is
reserved for the cloaked hound; `BESTIARY.md` §2.5 keeps 21100–21109 for the
first nine. **21110 and 21111** open the block §2.5 leaves "free for later
enemies"; `tools/make_ep2_enemy_overlay.py` refuses any number the mod's
`MAPINFO` already assigns.

**Why `replaces`.** Every bestiary class replaces its Doom counterpart so a
map can place either number (MOD-SPEC §6.1). The chaingunner and the
Mastermind are the two Doom roles left free (the General took the
Cyberdemon). No map of ours places 65 or 7, so with the overlay loaded
nothing changes in the campaign; `summon ChaingunGuy` and `summon
SpiderMastermind` give ours.

## 1. How to play them (the PM; D26)

```sh
python tools/build.py                                   # the mod, unchanged
python tools/make_ep2_enemy_overlay.py                  # -> work/overlays/sd-ep2-enemies.pk3 (+ .txt)
SD_ALLOW_ENGINE=1 MAP=RANGE tools/run.sh -file work/overlays/sd-ep2-enemies.pk3
# console (~):  summon SD_LegionGunner     summon SD_LibertyColossus
```

The overlay is a self-contained PK3, loaded after the mod: its own
`zscript.txt` (version 4.12 and four `#include`s under `zscript/sd_ep2/`),
its own `MAPINFO` (a `DoomEdNums` block only) and its own `LANGUAGE` (five
ids in `[enu default]`, `[ru]` and `[ca]`). GZDoom compiles every `ZSCRIPT`
lump and merges every `MAPINFO` and `LANGUAGE` lump of every loaded file, so
nothing of the mod is replaced and dropping `-file …` removes them. Its source
lives in `work/overlays/ep2-enemies/` (gitignored `work/`: it exists on the
Windows PC only until integration moves it into `src/`).

## 2. `SD_LegionGunner`, the heavy machine-gunner

An M1919 belt-fed gun at the hip. Doom's chaingunner holds the trigger
until he loses sight; ours fires **bursts** and breathes between them, and
the pause is the player's window to step out and shoot.

| Stat | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Health | **80** | `ChaingunGuy` 70, plus the weight of the gun: one IZh-58 barrel (avg 80) kills him about half the time |
| Radius / height / speed / mass | 20 / 56 / 8 / 100 | infantry parity: corridors are built for it |
| Pain chance | **150** (59 %) | a little under the trooper's 170 (66 %): the gun steadies him |
| Attack | hitscan, `random(1,5) × 3` = 3–15 (avg 9) per round | the chaingunner's own roll (`A_SD_Hitscan`, Doom's spread) |
| Burst | **4–6 rounds**, one every 5 tics (F 3 + E 2): 7 rounds/s | the chaingunner fires ~7.8/s with no end; ours ends |
| Pause | 20 tics after each burst, 10 tics of aim before the next | the tell and the window |
| Burst damage | 45 average if every round lands (it will not, at ±22.4°) | a trooper's blast is 27 |
| Faction / species | Legion / own species | infighting holds (BESTIARY 2.2) |
| Drops | **`SD_Ppsh`** | see 2.1 |

States, on the trooper's frames and tics: `Spawn` A B; `See` A–D at 3 tics;
`Missile` E 10 (aim, roll the burst) → `Burst` F 3 Bright (one round) / E 2
(next, or leave) → `Cooldown` E 20 → `See`; `Pain` G; `Death` H–L; `XDeath`
M–U; `Raise` L→H. A burst also ends when the target dies or leaves his sight
(`A_SD_GunnerNext`, the shape of Doom's `A_CPosRefire` with a count instead
of its random escape).

### 2.1 What he drops

The brief asks for PPSh-41 ammunition. The trooper drops his weapon (the
IZh-58, D35) and Doom's chaingunner drops his chaingun, so the gunner drops
**`SD_Ppsh`**: to a player who has one it is ammunition (half its 35-round
`AmmoGive`, 17 rounds of `Clip`, Doom's dropped-weapon rule; **NOT VERIFIED**
in our engine), to one who has not it is the gun. The level lever this
creates: a gunner placed before MAP04's PPSh-41 hands it over early. If that
is unwanted, the alternative is one line, `DropItem "SD_Clip"` (the
rifleman's 5 rounds, BALANCE §4.1); the CEO's call at integration.

## 3. `SD_LibertyColossus`, the Episode 2 boss

The Statue of Liberty perverted: chrome, a crown of spikes, a torch that is a
rocket and flame projector, the tablet carried as a shield. He waits on
MAP06's dais (`docs/levels/MAP06-red-square.md` §11) and his death opens the
exit gate.

| Stat | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Health | **4500** | the General's 3000 × 1.5 (brief) |
| Radius / height | 44 / 110 | 88 wide and 110 tall still pass MAP06's 128 × 128 gate passage |
| Speed / mass | 10 / 1000 | slower than the General's 12: a statue walking; unmovable |
| Pain chance | 20 | the General's: effectively unstunnable |
| Missile chance | `MinMissileChance` 160, `MissileChanceMult` 0.5 | the General's (the Cyberdemon's): he attacks readily at any range |
| Flags | `+BOSS +NORADIUSDMG +DONTMORPH +BOSSDEATH` | the General's, one for one: no splash damage, so the RPG-7 does not do the work |
| Scale | none since D123 | `SDGN` was drawn ×1.3 until `SDLC` existed (D116); `SDLC` is drawn at full size, so the line went at its install. The ×1.3 lives on in the Chrome Marshal (`SD_ChromeGeneral`, D123) |

**Three verbs**, chosen by distance every time he attacks
(`A_SD_ColossusChoose`, on a 0-tic frame at the top of `Missile`):

| Verb | When | What | Numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) **Rocket salvo** | farther than 320 | three `SD_ColossusRocket` from the torch (88 up, 20 to his right), one every 14 tics, each aimed | impact `random(1,8) × 10` = 10–80, avg 45; blast 64 over 96 units. Speed 20 (Doom's rocket), side-steppable. A Cyberdemon rocket is avg 90 + 128 |
| (b) **Fire jet** | within 320 | five `SD_ColossusFlame` blobs, one every 3 tics, each up to ±5.6° off | the ROKS-3's blob (`random(1,8) × 4`, avg 18, `Fire`) with 16 tics of life: 352 units of reach |
| (c) **Stomp** | within 192, 96 times in 256 (38 %), else the jet | a quake (intensity 3, 14 tics, felt to 768) and a wave on the floor: every player within 256, in his sight, standing on the floor, takes `random(1,8) × 5` (5–40) as `Melee` and is thrown 12 units/tic away and 6 up | a player more than 8 units in the air when the foot lands is not touched: jump it. Players only, so the wave never starts a fight with his escort |

Timings: rockets E 10 (the tell) → F 8 / E 6 / F 8 / E 6 / F 8 → E 16
recovery (56 tics); jet E 10 → F 3 × 5 → E 12 (37 tics); stomp Q 14 (the
foot goes up) → R 6 (lands) → R 14 (34 tics). `See` plays a footfall on
every walk cycle, as the Cyberdemon's `A_Hoof`.

**His death opens the exit.** GZDoom runs a monster's thing special in
`AActor::Die`, whoever killed it, so MAP06's `Door_Open (12, 16)` on his
thing works with no code here, exactly as for the General on MAP03 (MAP06
§11; **NOT VERIFIED** in play there either, check E7). `+BOSSDEATH` and the
final `A_BossDeath` make MAP06's belt-and-braces fix possible as well: a
MAPINFO `SpecialAction = "SD_LibertyColossus", "Door_Open", 12, 16`.

Death: `SDGN` H 10, I 10 (scream), J K L 10, M 10 (`A_NoBlocking`), N O 10,
P 30, P −1 (`A_BossDeath`). No summon (the General's verb), no `XDeath`, no
`Raise` (a boss is not resurrected).

**Against the arsenal** (BALANCE §7's ruler, every shot landing): 4500 HP is
375 TT-33 rounds, 57 IZh-58 barrels, 563 PPSh-41 rounds, 34 RPG-7 direct
hits (impact only: `+NORADIUSDMG`), 40 hand-Katyusha rockets (almost seven
full salvoes); the General's row is 250 / 38 / 375 / 23 / 27. MAP06's ammunition was counted against
the General's 3000 (MAP06 §10); level-designer recounts at integration.

### 3.1 The two projectiles

| Class | Derives from | Sprites (existing) | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SD_ColossusRocket` | `SD_Pg7Grenade` | `SDPG` A (8 rotations, inherited) in flight, `SDPX` A–C burst | speed 20, impact 10, its own burst (64 / 96), `Obituary ""` |
| `SD_ColossusFlame` | `SD_FlameBlob` | `SDFJ` A–B in flight, `SDFX` A–E (inherited) | 8 flight frames instead of 12, `Obituary ""` |

The empty obituaries leave the colossus's own line instead of the RPG-7's or

## 4. Sprites to draw (contract with `art-director` and the PM's Gemini frames)

Doom's convention: a frame letter per pose; rotations 1–8 (1 = facing the
viewer, clockwise) for anything the player walks around, rotation 0 for
death frames. Both carry asymmetric marks (the flag on one shoulder, the
gun at one hip, the torch in one hand), so **eight separate rotations, no
mirrored pairs**, as the trooper and the General. Offsets in a `grAb`
chunk, centred, feet on the floor line (MOD-SPEC §6.3).

| E | aiming, the gun levelled (also the pause between bursts) | 1–8 | 8 |
| F | firing: muzzle flash at the hip (`Bright`) | 1–8 | 8 |
| G | pain | 1–8 | 8 |

Projectiles: none to draw now (both reuse the RPG-7's and ROKS-3's). A

## 7. Open, said plainly

- Everything above is **NOT VERIFIED in the engine** (D26): the ZScript was
  written against g4.14.2's API as used elsewhere in the mod (`A_SD_Hitscan`,
  `A_SpawnProjectile`, `A_Quake`, `Thrust`, `DamageMobj`), never compiled.
  E1 is the first thing to read.
- The tint: GZDoom applies a `Translation` to a truecolour PNG by
  palettising it first; the brass and chrome may look coarser than the
  sprites do. Cosmetic, provisional, gone at integration.
- Balance numbers marked (H) in the code are first guesses, to be measured
  in the PM's play (T5, MOD-SPEC §10).
- The overlay source sits in gitignored `work/`: until integration, a copy
  lives only on the Windows PC.
