#### 2.3.1 Licence obligations coming with M2 (not a failure today)

- **Freedoom BSD, clause 2.** Freedoom's licence (modified BSD) requires
  that redistributions in binary form *reproduce the copyright notice, the
  list of conditions and the disclaimer* in the accompanying documentation.
  Today nothing of Freedoom's is packed (the placeholders are read from the
  player's IWAD), so nothing is owed. The moment the D61 hands (TT-33 R3-A1,
  the fist R3-A2, and every weapon built on them) enter `assets/`, the zip
  must carry that notice. `tools/release.py` today only demands a credit
  *name* for BSD rows (`tools/licences.py` line 71), not the notice text.
  The text is not in the repo (`iwads/` holds only `freedoom2.wad`), so
  getting Freedoom's `COPYING` needs either the Freedoom 0.13.0 release zip
  (if it is still on the PC) or a download (CEO permission).
- **SDXL references (Fooocus).** `tools/art/hand_ref.py` (HEAD, docstring)
  cuts the ice axe's hand **out of** the CEO's Fooocus/SDXL images, not
  just "from reference". When it ships, its LICENSES.md row must name the
  source (the CEO's own generations, the model and its licence); whether to
  say "AI-assisted" in the credits is a CEO call. NOT VERIFIED: the exact
  licence terms of the model the CEO used.
- **Impact font** (RELEASE.md §2.1, `docs/art/LICENSES.md` lines 45-57):
  `SDPOSTR1`, `SDPOSTR2`, `SDDOOR1` and the eight switches carry pixels
  rasterised from Monotype's Impact. The position "bitmap output is not
  covered by the font licence" is NOT VERIFIED by a lawyer. My
  recommendation, as the role RELEASE.md asks to decide: **ask the CEO for
  a one-line decision** (accept the risk for a free non-commercial fan
  release, or re-render with an OFL font). Re-rendering is not cheap here:
  text-bearing lumps are reproducible only on the Mac (CLAUDE.md), so on
  this PC it would be a new proposal batch and a new approval.

### 3.2 GZDoom 4.14.2's default controls (read, engine not started)

From the installed `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\GZDoom\gzdoom.pk3` (read with
Python's `zipfile`): `engine/commonbinds.txt` (all configurations: `1`-`7`
slots, `mouse1 +attack`, `tab togglemap`, `shift +speed`, `capslock
toggle cl_run`, `F2`/`F3` save/load, `F6`/`F9` quick save/load, mouse wheel
`weapprev`/`weapnext`) plus **one** of `engine/defbinds.txt` (WASD: `w s a
d`, `space +jump`, `x crouch`, `e +use`, `mouse2 +altattack`),
`leftbinds.txt` (arrows, keypad) or `origbinds.txt` (classic: arrows,
`ctrl +attack`, `space +use`).

Which one a fresh install gets: the CEO's `gzdoom.ini` on this PC has
`cl_defaultconfiguration=0` and its `[Doom.Bindings]` section (lines
768-849) is exactly commonbinds + **defbinds**: `W/A/S/D`, `E=+use`,
`Space=+jump`, `X=crouch`, `Mouse1=+attack`, and no binding for Ctrl or the
arrow keys. This is one machine's config, not a fresh one, so the claim
"defbinds is the default" stays **NOT VERIFIED until the sign-off run with a
fresh config** (item h of §2.5); but the README's three lines are wrong on
the only GZDoom we have measured.

Related, not a README item: `MAPINFO` does not forbid jump or crouch, and
Space jumps by default. No map review mentions jumping; a jump could skip
a lock or a ledge. Gameplay (P1, D60), but worth one sentence from the
level-designer before v1: allow, or `nojump`/`nocrouch` per map.
