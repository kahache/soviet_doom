Soviet Doom: enemy sound effects, drafts (F5)

2026-10-13 20:29, F5, sound-designer. tools/audio/gen_sfx.py, seed 1977, 44100 Hz mono, Vorbis q6. Plan, cards and rights: docs/sound/ENEMY-SOUNDS.md.

Drafts: nothing approved, nothing in the PK3. 35 lumps, every bestiary sound that is not a voice. The twelve voices are the CEO's and postponed (D37); until they come the enemies keep Freedoom's voices. Under eight of those voices goes a bed (8 here): the squelch, the gear, the collapse, the General's mechanism failing, built now so a take only has to be laid on top. Routes: C our synthesis; B a CC0 pack authorised by D36, always layered with C; A Freedoom's DSSLOP (BSD-3). Nobody has listened to these yet. The numbers are instruments; the verdict is the CEO's ear.

How to hear them in the game (the PM, D26)

.venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/audio/gen_sfx.py          # rebuild the drafts, the overlay and the report
.venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/build.py                  # dist/sovietdoom.pk3
SD_ALLOW_ENGINE=1 tools/run.sh -file work/audio/sfx/sd-enemy-sfx-draft.pk3 +map MAP02
# MAP02 holds the bestiary gallery. Drop the -file argument and the Freedoom stand-ins are back.
# That a second -file and a later SNDINFO override the mod's is what the music and weapon
# overlays rely on; NOT VERIFIED by the sound-designer, who does not start the engine (D26).
