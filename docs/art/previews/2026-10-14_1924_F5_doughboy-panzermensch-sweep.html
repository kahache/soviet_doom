TODAY. SD_PanzermenschSchwer inherits every state of SD_Panzermensch: same sprites, so in game the two are told apart only by the time they take to die. BESTIARY 2.7 planned a Translation (a palette recolour) once SDPM lands.

WHY NOT A TRANSLATION, AS PLANNED. A Translation remaps palette indices. Our sprites are truecolour PNGs and the mod ships no PLAYPAL, so GZDoom would remap them through Freedoom's palette: what the Schwer would look like is NOT VERIFIED and probably not our colours. The rig makes the alternative cheap: render the Schwer as a sprite set of its own (about 47 lumps, the same script, minutes of compute) under a new sprite name, and mod-engineer gives SD_PanzermenschSchwer its own States block (about 20 lines). The name (SDPS is not in BESTIARY 2.3's lists) is mod-engineer's to check against MOD-SPEC 6.3.

TWO CANDIDATES, on cell C. S1, Dunkelgelb: the plate in the late-war German armour yellow (khaki_olive 4-12), Panzergrau then Dunkelgelb as in 1943. It is the clearest tell, the Doom way (the Baron and the Hell Knight differ by colour), but it costs legibility: his value moves onto the concrete's (pixels that separate from SDCONC1 at 96 drop from 98 % to 33 %), and a light Reich brute leans towards the Legion's 'light faction'. S2, Schwer: still black, 12 % bigger, with both the spikes and the stacks. It keeps every number of the base cell, but size reads only side by side, and 12 % at an unknown distance is a weak tell.

RECOMMENDATION: S2, as its own sprite set, keeping the dark mass. If you want a tell that reads at a glance, the next step is S2 with Dunkelgelb only on the pauldrons and the helm (the mass stays dark); that is one parameter, not drawn here.

8 · Recommendation: Doughboy C, Panzermensch C

Doughboy

RECOMMENDED: C, the severed head. The lore joke of BESTIARY 7 (the thing kept the helmet and lost the body) made literal: a torn neck under the sphere, the spine hanging out of it, dark-red drip (D49). It is the only cell whose 16-px silhouette is more than an egg (the stump is a tail under the circle), and it is on the line the CEO has approved so far: grotesque, serious, bleeding dark red. Valid combination: C's stump under B's Brodie helmet, which gives the top a brim at 16 px as well.

B, the Brodie: the flat 'soup bowl' the AEF actually wore in 1917; 'Doughboy' is that war's word, the M1 pot is 1941. Its brim is the helmet tell that survives at 16 px. A is the brief taken literally. D puts the trooper's chrome M1 on him and a steel grille for a jaw (1950s chrome): the most 'Liberty Legion', but chrome on top breaks BESTIARY 10 rule 5 (dark helmet on top, for the lamps), and it has the lowest separation against the lamp flat (H3).

Panzermensch

RECOMMENDED: C, the walking Panzer. MAP01 puts him in Z10, the darkest room of the map (light 96), and BESTIARY 10 rule 3 says the brute disappears there. C answers it with the one thing that reads in the dark: a lit visor slit, two yellow eyes in a black slab. The exhaust stacks are its shoulder horns and the side skirts widen the hips, so the 'rectangle with horns' of BESTIARY 8 holds from the front and the back. The lit slit is one material and fits any cell: 'D with C's slit' is a valid combination if you prefer the man under the iron.

A is the brief: riveted black slab, spiked pauldrons, a visor slit, the red banner on his back with the D12 symbol (rotation 5). B puts a giant Stahlhelm on him, the Reich's helmet, but at 1x it reads as a rounded head more than as a Stahlhelm. D is the most brutal: a bald khaki skull with an iron mask bolted on, bare arms, bolts through the flesh that seep dark red.

MEASURED. 68-75 px tall (BOS2 is 69; the rifleman 56), 44 wide. Body luma 47-59, the darkest mass in the game as BESTIARY 2.1 wants, below the rifleman's 63-75. In Z10 at 96 he separates from SDCONC1 on almost every pixel (a black shape on lighter concrete) and from the SDSTEL1 dado on about 73 %.

What does not work, honestly

DOUGHBOY. (1) Size: BESTIARY 7 says 'the size of a man's torso', but the actor has Cacodemon parity (radius 31, a 62-unit hitbox). Drawn torso-sized (about 18 px), shots would hit where nothing is drawn, so he is drawn to his hitbox: 50 px across, as big as Freedoom's HEAD. If you want him smaller, his Radius shrinks with him (mod-engineer, balance). (2) Value: his mean luma (141-170) sits on SDCONC1's 147, so cells A-C have a mean gap of 0-4 in the hangar, the worst of any enemy. The mean misleads for a two-tone figure (light pink, dark helmet): 82-94 % of his pixels separate from the wall in every scene, and hue (pink on grey-blue) helps; NOT VERIFIED in engine. (3) At 16 px A and D are an egg: the 'flat notch on top' is not readable; only B's brim and C's stump survive. (4) The veins read at 1x as fine cracks, not as veins. (5) In profile (rotation 3) the hanging lip shows as a small knob.

PANZERMENSCH. (1) At 16 px the four cells are nearly the same shape (a rectangle with two horns); the choice between them is made at 1x, not at range. (2) In profile he is thin (23 px): plate seen edge on. He is a slab only from the front. (3) The contract's 'black plate' (true black and khaki_olive 0-3) reads as a very dark olive, not as a neutral black: those are the Reich's only dark colours. (4) C's lit slit: a GZDoom 'Bright' frame lights the WHOLE sprite, not the slit. For the slit alone to glow in the dark it needs a brightmap (GLDEFS, hardware renderer), NOT VERIFIED here and mod-engineer's. Without one it is yellow paint; the lineup at 96 shows that case, and it still reads, dimmer. (5) C's rust stacks may read at range as two clubs carried on his back. (6) The attack (G1) is the overarm moment with the shell over his head; the wind-up frames E and F are not drawn yet.

BOTH. Light is gen_enemy's stand-in (RGB x L/224), NOT VERIFIED against GZDoom. The Doughboy's hover height comes from memory of P_ZMovement, NOT VERIFIED. Nothing here has been in the engine.
