6 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT CHANGED. The first L0 fell onto his left side, and the chrome football pad, the broadest thing on him, held his chest and his whole body up off the floor: 39 px of corpse standing on a shoulder. Now he falls BACK over his heels and twists as he goes, so he lies on his back across the screen. On L every part of him is measured on the floor (section 3): the hips, the head and the arms touch it, the legs and the cuirass are within 2 px, and what touches spreads from his head to his boots. The head is turned to the side, the arms lie relaxed with the elbows a little bent, the knees are a little bent, the shotgun lies beside him and the pool spreads under his chest. 19 px tall on the floor instead of 39.

THE FRAMES THAT LEAD THERE. H (the hit) is unchanged. I: his knees go and he tips back a little. J: falling back, twisting towards you. K: he hits the floor, still a little bent, the limbs settling. The gib's last frames had the same fault (the legs fell onto their side and lay as a hump); they now fall back flat as L does, and U is a pair of legs lying on the floor beside the M1.

HONESTLY. The chrome cuirass is a rigid shell, so where it meets the floor it is the cuirass's lip that touches, not the soft middle of the back (about 1.5 px up). To lie flat the hips settle back a little towards the floor (a pose parameter, hip_z), which a real body does and a rigid rig does not. J is a 5-tic in-between: it reads in motion, less as a still. A-G are byte-identical to the set you saw.
