8 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT THIS IS. D65: cell C as chosen, the severed head with the spine hanging and the dark-red drip, drawn to its hitbox (50 px across, radius 31 actor). Every letter doughboy.zs names: A floats (Spawn and See), B C D the attack (it draws breath and the shell kindles in its throat, the mouth gapes with the shell white-hot in it, it spits), E F the pain (it jerks back screaming, a round's hole in its left cheek, then rights itself bleeding), at 8 drawn rotations; G-L the death at rotation 0. 54 lumps. No gib: the class has no XDeath. The sweep's views are its pixels byte for byte (SDDBA1, SDDBA3, SDDBA5, SDDBD1).

EIGHT ROTATIONS, NOT MIRRORED PAIRS. The figure is handed: the helmet sits askew to its right, the flag decal and the buckle are on one side, the wound on the other. A mirror would move them.

THE DEATH. G: the eye bursts while it is still in the air (the burst flies from the socket). The engine drops a dead floater (its +FLOAT and +NOGRAVITY go when it dies: what every Doom cacodemon visibly does, NOT VERIFIED in GZDoom here); the floor GIF lets it fall under Doom's gravity from its hover. H: it lands, splits and the helmet flies off; I-K it rolls back onto its crown and deflates; L lies face up as a flat sack in a wide pool, the socket and the mouth to the sky, the spine fallen out across the floor, the helmet upside down beside it. Measured (section 5): sack, spine and helmet each touch the floor; the body stands 13.87 units at most (the test: under 18); what touches spreads 71.8 units. Blood is G 7%, H 13%, I 20%, J 24%, K 27%, L 28% of the death frames' pixels.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. The attack's three frames differ mostly inside the mouth: at 1x B, C and D read as 'the mouth opens' more than as three moments. The pain's tilt is small at range. The corpse is seen from 27 degrees (the hound's approved corpse is seen from 37; from there the sack's top filled the screen), and it is still the biggest corpse in the game: the head is 50 units across (the hitbox), deflated it spreads to about 64, and the upside-down helmet beside it is the tallest thing on the floor (about 25 units). The helmet keeps no decal (a stamp cannot turn over with it). The veins still read as cracks (the sweep said so).

NOT VERIFIED. Outside the engine (D26): the light is gen_enemy's stand-in, rotations are rig_core's reading of r_things.c, the hover (28 units) and the fall are from memory of P_ZMovement. The PM's engine pass, with the draft overlay (MOD-SPEC 6.9), is pending.
