10 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT IS HERE. Cell E as the CEO chose it, on the rig, for every frame the actor's states name: 77 SDHS lumps (walk A-D, attack E-G and pain H at 8 rotations, death I-M and gib N-U at one), the 5 SDBL lumps of his bolt, and 16 proposed claw lumps (V, W) that no state shows yet. Frame A at rotation 1 is the sweep's cell E, unchanged (a test checks it pixel for pixel without the talons). All 8 rotations are separate lumps: the chrome is on his right and the armband on his left, so Doom's mirrored pairs would put the plate on the wrong side.

HOW HE MOVES. He limps on the chrome side: the right stride is shorter and the heavy shoulder dips when the right foot takes his weight (tilt 4.5 to 9.5 degrees through the cycle), and the cannon swings like a dead weight. The attack is one sequence for both attacks because the ZScript plays E F G for the claw and for the bolt: the cannon comes up (E), its core lights red (F), the bolt leaves with a recoil (G). He dies falling to his right, over the cannon, where the rifleman falls to his left: the two Reich corpses do not lie the same way.

BLOOD. Much more of it than the rifleman's D38 deaths, as D48 asked, and dark red as D49 decided ("rojo oscuro, nada de oliva"): a spray from the chest that lands as splats, a second jet from the entry wound towards the shooter, a wet patch that spreads down the coat and drips, the seam of the graft tearing, and a pool under the chest and the hip. The gib throws a fountain of 46 drops and 24 chunks, and the floor fills as they land. The red is the rifleman's own: flag_red 1-5 over black for drops and stains, 0-3 for pools, both darker than the armband (flag_red 4-11).

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. The talons on his left hand are two or three pale pixels at 1x. From the front (rotation 1) the raised cannon foreshortens to a disc, as the rifleman's rifle does. His chrome side (rotation 7) measures the lowest value gap of the set on green steel at light 112 (section 9): the cell's own 33 % chrome on that side, accepted in D48, not new here. Rotation order, grAb offsets and the additive bolt are checked by hand and in a stand-in, NOT in GZDoom.

For the CEO
  1. Approve the set as drawn (recommended): the 77 SDHS and 5 SDBL lumps go to assets/sprites/ and mod-engineer swaps TROO for SDHS and BAL1 for SDBL (MOD-SPEC 6.3, a rename), to be judged in GZDoom on MAP01.
  2. The claw: keep E F G for both attacks (today's states, recommended: it is Doom's imp, and nothing has to change in code), or ask mod-engineer to split Melee from Missile so the proposed V W claw frames play (a ZScript change, not a rename).
  3. Blood: look at section 7 beside the rifleman's red deaths. Both use the same red materials (and the Legion's), so they should read alike; if they do not, say which one is right and the other follows. The colour itself is D49's, and the actor's BloodColor already follows it (section 7's swatch).
