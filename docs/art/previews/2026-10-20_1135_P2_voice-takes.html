  • Casting: one voice per enemy, chosen by name and measured (pitch and brightness of its reference clip), without listening to it. Discarded for doubtful pitch (male/female overlap zone): Henry, Julian, Connor, Jeremiah. The preset voices come with the server and their origin is not documented (D87).
  • Processing: silence trimmed, low-cut filter at 90 Hz, and the Legion with a soft radio colour (the full radio chain, with crackle and squelch, comes later). Each take is at the volume of the Freedoom sound it would replace (the same rule as the weapons: the loudest 50 ms, A-weighting); the Freedoom player is above each line.
  • Faction chain not applied yet: the rifleman does not yet have the pitch drop or the saturation (REICH-DRY), nor does the General have his −4 semitones and the slot (GENERAL-SLOT). Judge the performance and the German/English, not the final timbre.
  • Longer than the script: the warning marks takes that exceed the planned duration; they can be sped up or trimmed when chosen.
  • Wordless (R07, R08, L07, L09, G03, G04): grunts and last breaths are not generated; they stay on the fallback effects.
  • Whisper heard: automatic transcription of each processed take with openai-whisper medium (local, en), in the language of the line, to see at a glance whether it is understandable. It is a machine, not a listener: your ear rules.
  • The log of each take (tool, model, voice, seed, settings) is in work/audio/voice_takes/AI-LOG.csv.
