6 · What you decide, and what the install does

  1. SDDL: option A, comet. It is the brief and it is already in the game: the approved SDDB B-D frames kindle exactly this white core in red in the mouth, so the shell leaving the mouth is the shell that flies. Measured against the storm bolt it is the bolt turned inside out (white centre, red rim; the bolt is red inside white) and a third larger. B, the roundel, is the most legible of the six and the most Legion, but it puts an insignia on a projectile (your call, D12); C, the spit, washes out pale on brick and reads as a puff, not a threat.
  2. SDPL: option A, ember. It is the brief and it is already in the game: the approved SDPM and SDPS E-G frames form and throw this ball in the right gauntlet. It is the largest flying shell (22 px against the bolt's 12), dull and olive-edged: heavier, as BESTIARY §8 asks. B, the grenade, is the best idea and the worst read: at 1x it is a thin stick, and it would cost 48 attack lumps redrawn; C, the slag, is busy at 1x and its lore holds only in MAP03.

The install, on your yes (mod-engineer and art-director; no tic moves): copy the five SDDL?0.png to assets/sprites/doughboy/ and the five SDPL?0.png to assets/sprites/panzermensch/; in doughboy_shell.zs and panzer_shell.zs rename BAL2 / BAL7 to SDDL / SDPL on the two state lines and the header comment (and doughboy.zs's comment); drop BAL2 and BAL7 from MOD-SPEC §5's IWAD sprites and mark them installed in §6.3, BESTIARY §2.7 / §7 / §8 / §12; ten rows in docs/art/LICENSES.md; tests/test_doughboy_sprites.py (SHELL_SPRITE = "BAL2") and tests/test_panzermensch_sprites.py move to the new names; then the PM's engine pass. Freedoom's BAL7 had rotations 1-8 for its flight; ours is rotation 0, one image for every angle, as SDBL and SDGL.

Or name another option per shell (A, B, C); its full set renders in one command.
