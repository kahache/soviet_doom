Reich rifleman — 3D rig, full frame set (F5, D38)

SDRR · 70 lumps · draft for the CEO's gate · 2026-10-13 20:39

D38: cell E (crested helmet, eyeless, calf-length greatcoat, bolt rifle at the hip) built as a 3D rig and rendered for the whole frame set Doom needs: walk, aim, fire and pain at 8 rotations, death and gib at one. About 50 primitives on a skeleton with IK legs and arms, sphere-traced in numpy at 16 rays a pixel, camera 9 degrees down, light fixed to the camera and cut into 5 bands, majority vote to 1x, palette v1 only, seed 38. Built by tools/art/gen_rig.py. Draft: nothing in assets/.

8 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT WORKS. The method does what it promised: 70 lumps from one figure, and every rotation, every walk frame and the death agree with each other, which 35 hand layouts would not. The legs stride under the coat in profile (rotations 3 and 7), the aimed rifle is a true profile at 3 and 7 and a three-quarter at 2, 4, 6, 8, and the fall reads: hit, knees, falling to his left, lying across. Value holds the faction contract: khaki_olive and black only, no military_green, the armband the only saturated thing; walking body luma 63 to 75 against the flat cell E's 65, worst gap 13 to 19 on SDSTEL1 at light 112 against E's 17 (11 on the firing frames, whose flash is counted; section 7). Beside Freedoom's zombieman and shotgun guy (section 6) he is the same height, reads at 1x and at game distance as a helmeted soldier with a red armband, and does not look like either placeholder.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. Up close (4x) he is a rendered mannequin, not a painted sprite: smooth rounded forms, no hand-placed highlights, the coat folds faint. The eyeless face is a plain light oval under the brim, which reads more as a mask than a face. From the front the fired rifle foreshortens to almost nothing and the flash sits on his chest (the flat sweep had the same problem). The walk at rotation 1 moves little, because the greatcoat hides the legs. Rotations 6 to 8 show the red band without the symbol (the band's outer face is turned away), and 4 and 5 show the symbol on a band seen edge-on, a convention. The gib reads weakest: the Reich's olive blood (BESTIARY 2.1) is dark on a dark floor, the chunks are blobs, and some land up to 3 px below the sole line (the dropped rifle of the death, 2 px).

NOT VERIFIED. Rotation order and grAb offsets are checked by hand against r_things.c, not in GZDoom; the sprites are RGBA PNG with hard alpha, like the fonts (ART-BIBLE 2 exception), and the shipped format is mod-engineer's call. The scene light is gen_enemy.py's stand-in.
