5 · Recommendation: C

The cells

RECOMMENDED: C, the gun on the shoulder. BESTIARY 9 and D35 say he orders, he does not brawl; C draws that sentence. His hand only points a swagger stick (the 'Vorwärts!' of his summon, D36), and the salvo comes from a field gun riding his right shoulder, so the attack frame reads as a command carried out, not as a big man firing a big pistol. It also gives him the one asymmetric shape in the bestiary: in profile (rotation 3) a long barrel over the shoulder, which nothing else carries, and at 16 px a notch on one shoulder that the Panzermensch's two horns never make.

A is the brief taken literally and already works: the hourglass the brief promises (cap wedge, narrow waist, coat flare), chrome on the chest and the face, the two pennants carrying the two insignia. B gives him a face (aviators and a corncob pipe: the Yankee general as the 1950s drew him); the most characterful at 3x, but at 1x it reads as a pink face, i.e. as a big trooper, and the chrome plate is what says 'not a man'. D wears the two flags as one cloak: the strongest back view (rotation 5 is the whole alliance in one picture) but from the front the cloak is two red-striped edges and the hourglass becomes a trapezoid. E, in dress uniform, is the most 'officer', but without the cuirass his chest is the coat's pale plaster and he loses the Legion's chrome: he reads as a statue.

Valid combinations, one parameter each: C with B's face; C with D's cloak (the gun is on the shoulder, the cloak hangs behind it); A or C with E's medals over the cuirass.

What does not work, honestly

(1) At 1x his flags are 7 px wide: the swastika is the armband's 5x5 drawing and the US flag the trooper's 7x5 patch, both hard-stamped; they read as 'a red flag and a striped flag', which is what the brief asks, not as detailed emblems. (2) The cap's braid and the epaulettes' fringe are a yellow line and a yellow edge at 1x; 'too much braid' reads at 3x only. (3) Rotation 1 of the firing frame points the revolver at the camera, so the gun is a short chrome blob under a flash (Doom's own convention: the Cyberdemon's arm reads the same way); C's gun is on the shoulder and its flash sits beside the head. (4) The coat is a rigid cone: in the walk frames the boots must swing under a hem that does not move; the full set will need a coat that sways (the hem tilted per frame), not drawn here. (5) He is HANDED (a different flag on each shoulder, the gun on one side), so his set costs eight drawn rotations per frame, not the five of Doom's mirroring that BESTIARY 9's 54 assumed: nine rotated frames (walk A-D, salvo E-F, pain G, summon Q-R) x 8 = 72, plus nine death frames, 81 images and the shell's 5. (6) Light is gen_enemy's stand-in (RGB x L/224), NOT VERIFIED against GZDoom; nothing here has been in the engine.

For the CEO
  1. Choose the General: C (recommended), A, B, D or E, or a combination named above (e.g. 'C with B's face').
  2. Then the full set is rendered on the rig (walk A-D, salvo E-F, pain G, death H-P, the summon Q-R at eight rotations; the shell SDGL A-E), for your gate in GZDoom.
