3. How the shot was built (no firing image)

  • Kick: SDAK B is SDAK A's very pixels moved 2 px right and 3 px up (the pipeline's KICK), exactly as every Gemini weapon's firing frame is placed (the PPSh, the TT-33 and the IZh kick by the same amount; Gemini drew no recoil in any pair).
  • Flash: the mod's own Gemini muzzle flash, the PPSh-41's (same chat style, same composition), cut from the PPSh pair by the same split, made whole (its left tip was cut by the image side and is tapered to a point; the notch the PPSh's barrel left is closed) and grafted at the AKM's slanted muzzle brake, the burst ahead of it as on the PPSh. The AKM's barrel end is drawn in front of the burst, as the PPSh's is. It rides on B with the kick, 2 tics, as akm.zs's Flash state plays it.
  • Colour: Gemini painted the laminate and the bakelite magazine a saturated red that snaps to the flags' red (flag_red) over half the rifle; the AKM's build keeps that ramp out, so the wood lands on marble_red, the reddish-brown of a real AKM's laminate.

6. Notes for the decision

  • Same height above the bar as the PPSh-41 (56 px ready, 59 firing) and the same width within a few px; the arms, sleeves and pixel size match the arsenal, as the TT-33's did.
  • The flash is the PPSh's, not one drawn for the AKM: same size and shape. A rifle's flash could be bigger or show the brake's sideways jets; that would need a new firing image (a Gemini ask) or a drawn variant.
  • Installing changes only sprite names in akm.zs (every tic, action and jump stays) and drops the PROVISIONAL-D116 tint line. The magazine pickup (SDAM, ammo.zs) keeps its provisional tint: it is not part of this set.
