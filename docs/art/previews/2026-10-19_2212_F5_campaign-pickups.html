Keys: ship the three icons below as graphics/STKEYS0-2.png (indexed, palette v2: STKEYS0 joins PALETTE_V2_LUMPS), which the status bar and the fullscreen HUD both draw. NOT VERIFIED in the engine.
  5. Palette v2: SDAH (option A) into APPROVED_IN_V2 in tests/test_palette_v2.py, as SDKB. Everything else is v1.

Decisions

For the CEO
  1. Choose A, B or C per object (the recommendation is on each card), or ask for a redo of one.
  2. The heavy armour in palette v2's cobalt, as the blue pass: yes or no (B shows v1's best blue).
  3. The red cross on the medical items. The art bible says nothing about it (checked); the emblem is protected by the Geneva Conventions and the ICRC has asked game makers not to use it on health pickups. Period-true for 1977 and common in mods; a swap (a white cross on red, or a red crescent) is one constant in the generator and a re-render. Keep the red cross, or swap.
  4. The Katyusha's pickup at its drafted size (A, 70x37 against Freedoom's 56x24) or at the footprint (B).
