Everything waiting for your ear to close M3: 31 weapon sounds, 35 enemy sounds, 8 layers under the voices and 6 pieces of music (plus 3 optional ones at the end). Each row: the draft, and below it what plays in the game today, for comparison. Press OK, NO or ? (I am not sure, I want to hear it in the game) and write a note if you like. It is saved only in this browser. When you finish, Copy result and paste it in the chat.

  • Judge the character, not the volume. Freedoom's sounds are 8-bit at full scale and play 4 to 16 dB louder than the drafts; pickups, reloads and empty clicks are quiet on purpose. The volume is adjusted afterwards.
  • Better with headphones. The sounds marked in amber have almost all their energy in the bass and are lost on laptop speakers.
  • Music: MIDI files do not play in the browser, so they are rendered to OGG with the same library (zmusic.dll) and the same soundfont (gzdoom.sf2) as your GZDoom, without starting the game. Checked: the output is identical to the render the engine made on the Mac. If a player fails, each piece has the command to open the MIDI with your own player.
  • Weapons with takes A/B/C (six): pick the take; A is the designed one, B heavier, C drier.
  • The 12 voices (rifleman, Legion, General) are not here: they are waiting for your takes (D37).
