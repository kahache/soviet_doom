7 · How it reads, and what is left

WHAT THIS IS. D50: the CEO chose the German shepherd, cell A, with the collar as drawn. This is its whole frame set, every letter SD_ReichHound's states name (src/zscript/monsters/reich_hound.zs, read by the generator for the timings): the gallop A-D (Spawn idles on A B), the bite E-G, the pain H, all at 8 rotations, and the death I-N at rotation 0. 46 lumps, MOD-SPEC 6.3's reserved SDHN. Mirror mode: the dog is symmetric and wears no insignia, so 2-4 are traced and the engine flips them into 8-6 (SDHNA2A8, ...). No gib: the class has no XDeath (BESTIARY 6) and the engine falls back to Death.

THE CORPSE. N lies on its right side, the whole length of it on the floor: chest, croup, neck, head and tail, all four paws. It is not posed by eye: the body is rolled onto its side and set on the floor, then the neck and head, the tail and each leg are let fall about their root until they touch it (the lower legs from the knee and elbow, the upper ones from the hip and shoulder, which is what leaves them scissored and loose). The test measures every part (section 6) and fails if any is propped up, the Legion's L0 fault.

THE VIEW OF THE DEATH. The rig's camera looks 9 degrees down, which is right for a standing figure; a dog lying on its side, seen from there, is a tan tube with its legs end-on. A player looks down at a corpse (eye 41 units up, 25-30 degrees at 64-96 units), so the death is drawn from 37 degrees, ramped in as it falls (I at 9, matching the gallop). The model the test measures is not tipped, only the render.

BLOOD (D49). Dark red, flag_red 0-5 and black only, as the rifleman and the Legion: the round opens the left side of the neck at I, the burst flies and lands as splats, the coat soaks, blood runs from the mouth, the pool spreads from L and is widest under N. The open mouth (bite, pain, the death's I-K) keeps the sweep's flag_red 2-8: it is the inside of a mouth, not blood.

SPEED. The GIFs run at the actor's tics: See is SDHN AABBCCDD at 2 tics, so each picture holds 4 tics (114 ms). The sweep's gallop GIF showed each picture 2 tics, twice as fast as the game.

NOT VERIFIED. All of it is rendered outside the engine (D26): the light is gen_enemy's stand-in, the rotation order is rig_core's reading of r_things.c, and the corpse's grAb follows the rifleman's (the near paws sit up to 11 px below the anchor; GZDoom's sprite clipping may lift it). The PM's engine pass is pending, with the draft overlay (MOD-SPEC 6.9).
