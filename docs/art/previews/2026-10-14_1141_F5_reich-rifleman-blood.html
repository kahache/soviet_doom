5 · Honest verdict and what you decide

THE FALL. D38's corpse lay on his left side, propped on his left shoulder and arm with the helmet under his head (the complaint the Legion's corpse got: "parece que se apoye en el hombro"). Now H and I are as approved, and from J he falls to his left while turning onto his back; at K he is down with the right leg still coming over and the Stahlhelm off; at L he lies flat on his back: shoulders, hips, both legs and the bare head on the floor (each within FLAT_TOL = 1.2 units, tested), the coat skirt flattened, the arms loose on the floor, the rifle and the helmet beside him on the floor, not sunk into it. The corpse is 20 px tall instead of 38. In the gib, the legs that stay now go down onto their back too and lie flat (U), and the helmet and the rifle rest on the floor level (D38 sank them up to 4 units and left the rifle propped on its butt).

WHAT IT DOES. Death: at H the round bursts out of his chest (36 droplets on ballistic arcs, a third thrown towards you) and the wound opens on the coat; the stain soaks wider every frame (half width 2.4 to 5.6 px), blood runs from the mouth (I-L) and down the coat front (J-L), the droplets land as splats around him, and a pool spreads under his chest on K and grows on L, with a smaller one under the head. Gib: a bigger burst (56 droplets), three pulsing jets from the stump while the legs stand (N-R), 30 chunks instead of 18, red gore, splats, and a pool that grows under the legs until U. Every blood colour is flag_red 0-5 and black from palette v1, darker than the armband (flag_red 4-11 unshaded), so the symbol still reads and nothing needs palette v2.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. Doom draws a floor pool on a sprite, and our camera looks down only 9 degrees, so the pool is a band 3 to 5 px deep in front of the body, not a puddle seen from above; it is pushed forward of the body (POOL_Z) to show at all. For the same reason a man flat on his back shows his side, so the chest wound reads as a red line along his top edge on L, not a patch. At 1x the droplets are 1-2 px dots: they read as spray in motion, as Doom's own do, and as noise on a still. Frame H holds the burst for 5 tics only, so the strongest read of the hit is GZDoom's own blood puff, not this sprite (next paragraph). The light stand-in is NOT VERIFIED against GZDoom.

THE REST OF THE GAME. Today GZDoom sprays Freedoom's blood when you hit him (BLUD, red; the 2026-10-13 23:10 overlay evidence shows it), because SD_ReichRifleman sets no BloodColor. That red is a pure, more saturated red of about the same darkness (BLUD averages RGB 115, 0, 0; our mid tone is 97, 22, 19; section 3), so the dark-red death sits in the same family and does not fight it, where an olive death would have clashed with a red hit puff. To make hit and death one colour, mod-engineer can set BloodColor on SD_ReichRifleman to a dark red near flag_red 3, RGB (97, 22, 19); BloodColor recolours the stock blood (BESTIARY §2.1 says so; the syntax and the on-screen result are NOT VERIFIED here, and are mod-engineer's and the PM's engine pass). BESTIARY §2.1 ("Blood: dark olive") and §3 ("Olive blood") must be amended to dark red for the Reich.
