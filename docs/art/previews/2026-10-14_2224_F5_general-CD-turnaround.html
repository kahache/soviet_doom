6 · How it is built, what does not work, what next

WHAT KEEPS THE CLOAK OUT OF THE FRONT. It is D's cloth (the same half cone, the Reich's red and the white disc on his left half, the stars and stripes hung vertically on his right) cut in two and trimmed: below the waist to the coat's width less 1.2 units, above it to the cuirass's, so from the front it is always inside his own outline. Its bottom stops 1.5 units above the coat's hem (the hem hides it; the camera looks down 9 degrees, so the back of the cloak shows a little HIGHER than the coat's edge, never lower), and its top at 71, under the pauldrons, so it never peeks over the shoulders. It is flatter than D's (depth 0.72 of its width against 0.85): it stands about one unit off the coat's back at the hem, which keeps rotations 2 and 8 to a sliver. The pennants hang in front of the shoulders and the cloak behind the back: they never meet.

MEASURED (section 3; tests/test_gen_general.py holds it). At rotation 1, standing and firing, not one pixel of the cloak shows; the silhouette is C's, the same width, the hourglass untouched. The sprite differs from C's in 4 pixels (9 when firing), all shading: the hidden cloak, close behind the coat's edge, darkens the renderer's ambient occlusion there by one ramp step. Rotations 2 and 8 show its edge as a 2-3 px strip behind the coat's back edge, about 100 px of cloth: what a cloak seen three-quarters from the front shows; the silhouette stays the hourglass. From 3 to 7 it is D's cloak: the whole alliance across his back at 5.

SWAY (a note, not drawn). In the full set the coat's hem and the cloak should swing with the walk: the hem tilted a few degrees against the leading leg in each of A-D, the cloak's lower segment hinged at the waist and lagging one frame behind the hem; in the death frames the cloak falls with him and spreads on the floor (the corpse flat, as D55). Both are pose parameters on the rig, no new method.

HONEST. (1) The trim makes the cloak narrower than D's: from the back he is the cloak plus a band of chrome back-plate and the pennants above and beside it, where D's covered him shoulder to shoulder. (2) The canton's stars are a 1-px checker at 1x. (3) In profile (3, 7) the cloak's trimmed side is a flat face of stripes or red, a panel behind the coat. (4) From the back the red half sits on the portal hall's red brick: only 58 % of his back's pixels separate from SDBRCK1 at 128 (85 % from the front); the chrome, the cap and the white stripes carry his outline there, and against the drum's steel and the portal he separates on 72 % and 96 %. (5) When he fires, the cloak stays with his hips while the chest turns 8 degrees (so its edge never swings out past the coat at F1). (6) Light is gen_enemy's stand-in, NOT VERIFIED against GZDoom; nothing here has been in the engine.
