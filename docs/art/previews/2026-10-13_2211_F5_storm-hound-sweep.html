8 · Recommendation, the rig, and what you decide

Stormtrooper

RECOMMENDED: B. The chrome coil on his right shoulder is the silhouette BESTIARY 5 asks for: one shoulder a bright blob, the other a dark slope, the only unbalanced humanoid in the bestiary. A's forearm cannon alone is a thin pale stick that at 1x reads as a rifleman holding something; C adds a pauldron, a US patch and a hose and goes over the 25 % rule (27 % on his right side) for detail that is invisible at 1x; E's chrome skull plate is striking up close but puts him at 33 % and makes his head the brightest thing on him, which fights the shoulder tell.

THE HONEST PROBLEM: at 1x, B still reads as a rifleman with a silver arm. Same crested helmet, same greatcoat, same value (body luma 70-74 against the rifleman's 63-75). D is the cell that separates best from the rifleman at range: the tunic shows his legs and the gas mask changes the head, so the two Reich humanoids stop sharing a silhouette. Suggestion if you want him further from the rifleman: B's coil on D's body (tunic, breeches, gas mask). It is a combination, not a new cell: ask for it.

MEASURED. B's Legion share is 17 % from the front and 26 % from his right side: half a point over the rule, fixed by trimming the coil housing about 10 % in the next round. Worst value gap 15 on SDSTEL1 at light 112, the rifleman's own range. Palette v1 has no pink ramp (v2 reserves it), so the 'pink flesh at the seam' is plaster_warm with a rust_brown raw edge; at 1x it is a pale pixel or two. The armband stays on his Reich (left) arm; BESTIARY 5 does not mention it, and it is the faction tell.

Hound

RECOMMENDED: D. The crocodile jaw and the bony dorsal spines are what stop him being a hairless greyhound: A, the brief taken literally, reads as an Earth dog, which BESTIARY 6 says he is not. The spines break the top line of the horizontal bar, so he is named at range even in the dark. B (hyena) and E (mastiff) are the most alien builds; B's sloped back with D's head and spines is a valid combination. C's harness ties him to the Reich without insignia but adds nothing at 1x.

MEASURED. Profile 33 px tall (rule: under 40) and 58-75 px long, a horizontal bar beside the Freedoom placeholder's vertical worm. Black bottom edge on 75-83 % of the columns, so the belly line reads over SDFLR1's lane (S6, BESTIARY 10 rule 4). Worst gap 13-15 on SDSTEL1 at 112: the 'caution' of BESTIARY 10, same as the rifleman.

A DEVIATION, STATED. BESTIARY 6 gives the hide as khaki_olive 4-10. The first render in that range measured body luma 99 against SDSTEL1's 101: a gap of 1, the hound vanished on green steel. Every cell here uses khaki_olive 1-9, low on the ramp, Reich-dark like the rifleman (body luma 70-75). No red anywhere, as BESTIARY 6 asks; teeth and spines are plaster_warm, the gullet rust_brown.

FREEDOOM, LOOKED AT. BESTIARY 5 and 6 left it NOT VERIFIED: TROO is a tentacled serpentine creature, not a humanoid, and SARG is a worm, not a quadruped. Neither is a base for these two; both are drawn from scratch, and the placeholders are shown for scale only.

The rig and the quadruped

THE STORMTROOPER: YES, AS IS. He is the rifleman's own rig model with the rifle and his right arm taken away and the graft added, so the 53 lumps of BESTIARY 5 are the rifleman's pipeline with new poses. Two small things would make it clean rather than clever: gen_rig tags no limb by side (the right arm is found here by its position, x < 0), and its material table is closed (the new materials are appended to it at import time). Both are a few lines in gen_rig.py, reported, not done.

THE HOUND: THE RENDERER YES, THE SKELETON NO. Everything that draws (primitives, sphere tracer, banded light, majority vote, contour and rim, palette, lineup on MAP01) served a quadruped unchanged. What a quadruped needs and the rig does not have: (1) a skeleton with a hierarchy: a spine chain pelvis-chest-neck-head that bends and carries the legs; gen_rig rotates groups about one pivot each and has no parent-child bones; (2) a three-bone leg: the hind leg is digitigrade (hip, knee, hock, paw); gen_rig solves two bones only, and this sweep holds the metatarsus at a fixed angle, which is fine standing and wrong in a gallop; (3) a quadruped pose: four paw targets with gait phase, spine flex and body pitch per frame, where gen_rig's Pose is two feet and a rifle; (4) its own canvas and falls: gen_rig's canvases are a standing man's and its death rolls a biped to his left, a hound falls on its side along its length; (5) a generic render(prims, rotation, canvas): gen_rig's render() is wired to the rifleman, gen_storm.py carries its own.

AND ONE SAVING. The hound has no insignia and is symmetric, so Doom's mirrored rotation pairs (5 drawings per frame, BESTIARY 6's count of 46) are valid for him; gen_rig always renders all 8, which the rifleman and the Stormtrooper need (their armband) and the hound does not. A mirror mode saves about a third of his lumps.
