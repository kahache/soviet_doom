6 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT CHANGED. The first M0 rolled onto his right side, and the chrome shoulder and the cannon, the bulkiest things on him, held his chest off the floor: a corpse leaning on its arm. Now he falls BACK over his heels and twists towards his chrome side as he goes, so he lies on his back across the screen. On M every part of him is measured within 2 units (= px) of the floor (section 3): the head, the cannon arm, his left arm, the shins and the boots touch it, the greatcoat and the hips settle onto it, and what touches spreads from his skull to his boots. The cannon arm lies flat beside him on the side you see, the plated half of the skull turned up, the left arm loose at his side, the pool under his chest and hips.

THE FRAMES THAT LEAD THERE. I (the hit) keeps its pose; now the whole head, chrome plate and all, tips back together (before, only the face did). J: his knees go and he tips back. K: falling back, twisting. L: he hits the floor, still settling. The gib had the same fault in its last frames (the legs fell onto their side): they now fall back flat as M does, and U is the coat and the legs lying on the floor beside the skull and the cannon arm. The blood is unchanged: D49's dark red, as much of it.

HONESTLY. The greatcoat's skirt is deeper than his chest, so where he lies the coat's back and the torso sit a little under 2 units up while the head, arms and boots touch; a real coat would flatten, the rig's does not. To lie flat his legs settle back inside the coat (a pose parameter, hip_back), which a body does and a rigid rig does not. Walk, attack and pain (A-H, 64 lumps), the bolt and the GIFs of A-H are byte-identical to the set you approved. Light is gen_enemy.py's stand-in, NOT VERIFIED against GZDoom.
