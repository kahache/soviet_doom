What I need from you

  1. Yes / no per piece. Each card is independent; a no costs one card, not the set.
  2. The tunnel: concrete (SDTUNL1), tubbing (SDTUBB1), or both (tubbing for the deep stretches, concrete near the stations). My recommendation: both.
  3. The platform edge: the painted line on SDMFLR3 is period-plausible but the chevrons on SDMEDG1 are an industrial idiom, not a 1970s metro one. They are there for gameplay (the edge must read from the track in the dark). Keep, or plain dark face?
