9 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT WORKS. Cell C as you chose it, now in every frame Doom asks for: the front, three-quarter and side views and the firing frame are the sweep's pixels, byte for byte, and everything else is the same figure posed, so the 70 lumps agree with each other. The chrome football pads, the round M1 and the shotgun high across the chest read at every rotation; from behind (4 to 6) he is a chrome back under a chrome bowl, still a T. He walks faster than the rifleman (3 tics a frame, ShotgunGuy parity) and the legs show because there is no greatcoat over them. Beside the rifleman on MAP01 the two infantries cannot be confused: light and broad against dark and narrow, chest gun against hip rifle.

THE BLOOD (D48, D49). The deaths bleed as the rifleman's: dark red, flag_red 0-5 and black, a burst out of the chest at H, a stain soaking wider on the chrome, blood from the mouth and down the cuirass, splats, a pool that grows on K and L; the gib bursts through the cuirass, jets from the stump while the legs stand, throws 30 chunks of meat and chrome shards, the M1 and the shotgun fly whole, and the floor fills. On the Legion the red reads better than on the rifleman: dark red on chrome and pale khaki is the strongest contrast he has. The flag stays the brightest red on him (flag_red 11).

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. The 'heavy' of BESTIARY 4 is still more promise than fact: he is slimmer than Freedoom's shotgun guy even with the pads (the sweep said so; you chose C as drawn, so the build was not touched). Up close (4x) he is the rig's rendered mannequin, as the rifleman. The firing frame from the front hides the face behind the flash. The pain frame is a lean back, not a flinch of the arms. The pool is a band a few pixels deep in front of the body, not a puddle seen from above (the camera looks down 9 degrees), and at 1x the droplets are 1-2 px dots: spray in motion, noise on a still. In the gib the flag goes with the chunks: the legs that stay carry no insignia.

NOT VERIFIED. Rotation order and grAb offsets are gen_rig's, checked by hand against r_things.c, not in GZDoom; the scene light is gen_enemy.py's stand-in, re-quantised to palette v2; the lumps are RGBA PNG with hard alpha like the rifleman's, and the shipped format is mod-engineer's call.
