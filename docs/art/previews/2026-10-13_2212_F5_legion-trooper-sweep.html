5 · Does gen_rig.py serve him? What it would need

YES, THE METHOD SERVES HIM, AND THESE SIX CELLS PROVE IT: every cell here is gen_rig.py's primitives (ellipsoids, round cones, capped cones, boxes), its two-bone IK for legs and arms, its rotations, camera, Freedoom reader, grAb convention and canvases, imported unchanged. Doom's SPOS layout is the same 21 frames as POSS (BESTIARY 4: same budget, 49 images), so gen_rig's frame plan, death fall and gib machinery carry over as they are.

WHAT GEN_RIG.PY LACKS (to be generalised before the full set, not edited in this task): (1) a chrome shading. Its banded Lambert makes chrome grey plastic; this sweep reflects a sky/horizon/ground environment with a hard specular dot, about 15 lines. (2) Materials as a parameter: its reduce() and to_rgba() read gen_rig's own MATERIALS, so a second figure cannot use them; this file carries copies that take the materials. (3) VALUE_SHIFT -0.26 and the NO_SHIFT/CLOTH sets are the Reich's; the Legion is the light faction and needs none. (4) build_model() is the rifleman; the figure should be a per-enemy function on a shared skeleton. (5) The armband stamp is hard-wired (stamp_symbol calls gen_enemy.stamp_armband); the Legion needs the US flag, mirrored so the canton leads. (6) The carry: rifle_frame knows hip and aim, the trooper needs 'high across the chest' and a pump. (7) The gib's CHUNK_MATS are olive; the Legion bleeds pale plaster_warm 10-14 (BESTIARY 2.1). (8) Tics: SPOS walks at 3 tics a frame and fires 10/10/10 (BESTIARY 4), the GIFs hard-code POSS's 4 and 10/8/8. (9) gen_enemy.lit() re-quantises scenes to v1, which would erase the pink until palette v2 exists.

PROPOSAL: once a cell is chosen, split gen_rig.py into a shared core (march, reduce, rgba, stamps, frame plan, GIFs, sheet) and one figure module per enemy, so the Legion's 49 lumps and the next three enemies of D35 are figure modules only. That is a refactor of an approved tool, so it is the PM's to commission, with tests/test_gen_rig.py as its tripwire.

6 · Recommendation: C. Honest verdict and what you decide

WHY C. The chrome football pads are the one change that makes him read as the Liberty Legion at every distance: they give BESTIARY 4's tell (wider at the shoulders than the rifleman) to the shape, not only to the colour, and they are American without a word or a symbol. Under them he keeps the brief: chrome M1 pot, chrome muscle cuirass (a Roman legion's armour for a 'Legion' of 'Liberty'), bare pink arms, the shotgun high across the chest. The smooth eyeless face pairs him with the eyeless rifleman: both factions are alien, neither has eyes you can meet. F (the same pads with the grin) is the valid alternative if you want him more inhuman; the grin reads at 1x, but it sits close to a cartoon and D11 asks for serious, so not as the default. D's visor is barely a band at 1x; E (bare torso) is the pinkest and the least armoured, closest to a man in trousers; A and B are C and F with smaller shoulders.

WHAT WORKS. Value, the faction plan: the light mass (median luma about 181 in every cell, khaki trousers 187, chrome about 180, flesh about 182) sits well above SDCONC1 (147) and far above our Reich rifleman (about 70): the two infantries cannot be confused, and at 16 px the trooper is a T (wide shoulders, legs apart) where the rifleman is a bell (the greatcoat). The faction contract holds: steel_blue chrome with its middle skipped, pink flesh, no military_green, no khaki_olive, the flag the only flag_red. Beside Freedoom's placeholders he is the same height and reads as a figure of his own.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. He is slimmer than Freedoom's shotgun guy even with the pads; 'heavy' is more a promise than a fact, and a wider rig (bigger thighs, a thicker waist) is one parameter round. The mean body luma (about 164, the black boots and the gun pull it down) gives a worst gap of about 11 on SDCONC1 at light 144, lower than the rifleman's 13 to 19 on SDSTEL1: he separates by being lighter than a light wall, which is the narrower margin; under the hangar's 176 to 192 the gap grows. The flag is 7x5, larger than a real patch and partly off the shoulder in the front view, on purpose so it reads (the rifleman's armband has the same convention). The firing frame from the front hides the face behind the flash, as the rifleman's does. Up close (4x) it is the rig's rendered-mannequin look, the same quality the CEO accepted for the rifleman.

THE PALETTE. Palette v1 has no pink; the sweep uses a candidate ramp for 16 reserved slots (section 4). In v1 only, plaster_warm stands in: he becomes a cream-skinned man, the flesh and the trousers merge, and 'pink flesh' (LORE, BESTIARY 2.1) is lost. Recommendation: add the ramp as palette v2.

For the CEO
  1. Pick a cell (recommended C; F if you want him more inhuman). Fixes can be named on top, each a parameter: a heavier build, a different face on C's body, the flag smaller or on the chest.
  2. Approve the pink: the candidate flesh ramp becomes palette v2 (16 of the 94 reserved slots, no v1 colour changes). Without it the Legion is cream, not pink.
  3. Then the full set (walk, aim, fire, pain at 8 rotations; death and gib) is rendered on the rig, as the rifleman's was, for your gate in GZDoom.
