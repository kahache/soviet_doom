SOVIET DOOM · THE LAST FREEDOOM PIECES OF THE SHELL (T12)

Menu cursor M_SKULL1/M_SKULL2 and pause sign M_PAUSE. Sweep 2026-10-13_2208, tools/art/gen_cursor.py, files in work/art/shell_extra/. Proposals only: nothing is in assets/.

Recommendation

  • Cursor: A, the red star. It is the one shape that reads at 17 px on both screens (cream items on the dimmed title, red rungs on the skill screen), it is already the mod's emblem (SDSTAR1, the title lockup) and its blink — the facets brighten and the gold rim flares to cream — is as calm as the skull's. The gold rim and a dark contour are what keep a red star readable on the red title. Runner-up: B, gold on red reads best of all, but at 17 px the hammer and the sickle start to merge. C and D are red on red and read as a UI arrow and a flag; E (Sputnik) is the most ours but its grey sphere is weak on the title.
  • Pause: P2, the word on a red plaque, one picture per language. GZDoom g4.14.2 can swap a picture by language (localized/textures/, read in gzdoom.exe), as long as both have exactly the same size — so both are drawn on one canvas. The plaque keeps the word legible on any wall. The file name the loader wants is NOT VERIFIED (engine source not on this machine): one engine pass settles it. If it does not work, P1 (the icon) needs no localisation at all and is the fallback.
  • M_EPISOD is not swept: D41 already replaced it with a text header.

1 · Menu cursor — five options, two blink phases each

20×20 canvas (Freedoom's skull is 20×19). The engine draws it at Selector "M_SKULL1", -32, -5 from each item and blinks M_SKULL1/M_SKULL2 every 8 tics (gzdoom.pk3 animdefs.txt); the contexts below blink at that rate (0.457 s per cycle). Menu: the real items in SDMENU read from assets/fonts/sdmenu/, TITLEPIC dimmed as the engine dims it, M_DOOM at 60,0. Skill screen: today's MENUDEF positions (header 32,70; rungs from 32,90). Notes

  • Every picture is RGBA with hard alpha and opaque pixels from docs/art/palette.json only (the ART-BIBLE §2 exception, like M_DOOM and the fonts). No system font is used: the words are our own glyphs, so the pictures are byte-identical on the Mac and on this PC (D27).
  • The menu colours are the same approximations as the D31 sheet: the engine's Cream/Gold translation of SDMENU is NOT VERIFIED until an engine pass; the cursor itself is drawn untranslated, so it is exact.
  • B reuses SDSTAR1's approved hammer-and-sickle geometry (gen_soviet_base.hammer_sickle), shrunk; it is not re-traced.
  • Freedoom's pictures are shown for comparison only (BSD licence, read from the IWAD).
