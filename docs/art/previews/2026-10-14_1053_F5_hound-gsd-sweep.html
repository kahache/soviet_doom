7 · How it reads, and the recommendation

WHAT CHANGED. The first sweep drew an alien: a hairless olive hide over ribs, a flat head that was mostly jaw, spines down the back. D48 asked for a German shepherd, and everything is new except the bones: the skeleton is rig_core's (spine chain, three-bone hind leg, transverse gallop), re-proportioned to the breed: the withers the high point and the top line sloping to the croup, a deep chest to the elbow and a tucked belly, the hind paw set well behind the hip. On it: a wedge head with a marked stop, skull and muzzle about equal, black nose and mask, tan pips over the eyes; large erect triangular ears; a strong neck with a pale ruff; broad thighs with breeches; a long bushy tail in a sabre, lifted in the run; the black blanket over the back and flanks, the rest fire tan with a paler underside.

HOW IT READS, HONESTLY. In profile at 1x it reads as a German shepherd at first glance, on every wall: the ears, the black saddle on tan and the sabre tail carry it. From the front (rotation 1) it is a narrow tan column, 14 px wide, with two ear points and a dark muzzle: a dog, a shepherd if you know to look. From behind (rotation 5) the black back and the tail dominate. That is the limit of a figure this small; Doom's own pinky has it too. The coat is suggested by the rig's grain and the ruff, not drawn hair by hair: at 3x the dog looks smooth-coated, at 1x nobody can tell. The gallop is rig_core's gait numbers, chosen by eye, not taken from footage.

THE GREEN STEEL (S3, SDSTEL1 at light 112), MEASURED. Cell A's MEAN body luma (88) sits close to the wall's, so the first sweep's measure gives a gap of only 6.7 at S3, smaller than the rejected cell D's 12.6. But the dog is not one value: 57 % of its pixels clear the wall by 16 luma or more (the black blanket below it, the cream above it), and the tan is orange on green, the complementary hue. On the 1x strip it is the most visible figure on S3, more than the rifleman. Cell B (browner, more black) has the best mean gap (17.3). The mean-luma measure underrates a two-tone animal; the hue split is NOT VERIFIED under GZDoom's light, which darkens with distance. BESTIARY §10 rule 1 (no Reich ambush in SDSTEL1 at light 112 or less) should stand until the engine pass. Rule 4's fallback, a true-black underbelly, is gone: a shepherd's underside is pale, so the map-side half of rule 4 (no lane line through a hound ambush) must hold on its own.

THE REICH TOUCH. A black leather collar with a steel tag at the throat, on every cell; at 1x the tag is one light pixel under the chin, seen from the front. Cell D adds the graft: a riveted steel plate on the skull and a stapled scar on each thigh, symmetric so the mirrored rotations stay legal. At 1x the plate is a light dot between the ears and the scars barely register: the most the dog can wear and still read as a shepherd first. No faction red: the only red is inside the open mouth (bite E and F).

HEIGHT. Side and front stay under BESTIARY §6's 40 px (34 and 31). Seen from behind, the camera's 9-degree pitch lifts the far head and drops the near hind paws, so the back view measures 40-42 px, as the first gallop proof's did (40). It still reads as a horizontal animal.

RECOMMENDATION: cell A, the classic black and tan, with the collar. It is the shepherd everyone pictures, its tan is the brown the CEO asked for, and its saddle gives the silhouette a dark top edge. If the CEO wants it browner, B is the same dog darker, and it measures better on green steel; C is the pale working line, the weakest on concrete (37 % legible) and on green steel by the mean (gap 4.8); D is A with the graft, for a Reich mark on the dog itself.
