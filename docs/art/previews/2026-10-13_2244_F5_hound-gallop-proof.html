6 · What this proves and what is left

WHAT THIS PROVES. The rig now has the three things the hound sweep said it lacked (its sheet, section 8): a spine chain (sacrum, loin, back, chest, then two neck bones, each angle relative to its parent), a three-bone digitigrade hind leg (hip, stifle, hock, paw, solved as a pantograph: femur and metatarsus stay parallel, every bone keeps its length, the stifle points forward and the hock back), and a quadruped pose with a gait phase (four paw targets, spine flex, bob and pitch from one number). The four frames are one transverse gallop: A gathered (hind paws reaching under the chest, back rounded), B the hind push, C stretched (forepaws reaching, back extended), D the forepaws landing.

MIRROR MODE. The hound wears no insignia and is symmetric, so Doom's paired rotations are valid for him: rotation 7 here is not traced, it is rotation 3 flipped about the body axis, exactly what the engine draws for a lump named SDHNA3A7. Five drawings per frame instead of eight: the full set is BESTIARY 6's 46 images, not 70. The flip swaps the lead leg (his left side leads at 3, the right seems to lead at 7), as every Doom monster's walk does; the light also flips (key light from the right at 7). Both are the convention, visible only if you look for them.

WHAT IS NOT DONE, HONESTLY. This is the recommended cell D's shapes moved onto the new skeleton, not a redesign; the body pieces are the sweep's ellipsoids hung on bones, so in the stretched frame C the loin and the ribcage show their seam, and the chest does not deform. The gait numbers (duty, stride, lift, flex) are chosen by eye for 4 frames at 2 tics, not taken from footage. Not rendered: the bite E-G, pain H, death I-N, and rotations 2, 4 (8 and 6 would be their mirrors). Rotation order and the flip are checked by hand against r_things.c, NOT in GZDoom.
