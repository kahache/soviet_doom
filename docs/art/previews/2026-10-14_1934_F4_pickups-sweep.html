Decisions

For the CEO
  1. Choose A or B per object (the recommendation is marked on each card), or ask for a redo.
  2. The pass in palette v2 (cobalt, D45), as the Legion is: yes or no. In v1 the only blue is steel_blue, a grey.
  3. The radio on a tabouret: the actor is 32 wide and 28 high, a waist-high body, so the set stands on a stool rather than being drawn a metre wide. Yes, or another piece of furniture.
  4. On approval: the mod-engineer reserves the names in MOD-SPEC 6.3 (only SDRD is reserved today), changes each Spawn state in zscript/sd/ammo.zs and radio.zs, and gives MAP03's pass a BlueCard subclass with frames A and B; the PM's engine pass first. The backpack (BPAK) is not in this sweep.
