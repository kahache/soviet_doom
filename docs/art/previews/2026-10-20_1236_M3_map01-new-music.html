What it is (D92). A new, original piece composed for the first map, in the style of D_SDM01T (the melodic part you liked): the same horns carrying the melody, the same General MIDI orchestra that plays in GZDoom (synth bass, cellos, tremolo strings, trombones, horns, cold pad, glass) plus timpani. But with combat drive: the melody enters in the first bar, underneath a rhythm never stops (galloping bass, cellos in eighth notes, timpani and a marching snare) and it returns to the start without a cut.

Form (48 bars, D minor, two versions with the same notes):

A · Theme1-16The horns sing the theme from the first beat; underneath, galloping bass, cellos in eighth notes, timpani on 1 and 3, marching snare. Cymbal on entry.
B · Advance17-32Moves to F major: the trombones call and the horns answer; then all together in octaves, with tremolo strings and a denser snare. An E flat chord brings it back to tension.

Rendered with GZDoom's own synthesizer (zmusic + gzdoom.sf2), without starting the game. Each audio keeps going for 10 s after the loop point: what plays after the roll is the beginning again, so you can hear the seam. Volume matched to D_SDGATE (Tankograd), the loudest approved combat piece. Use the buttons to jump to each section.
