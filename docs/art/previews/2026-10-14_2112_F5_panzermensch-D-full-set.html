8 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT THIS IS. D65: cell D as chosen, the bare khaki skull under a riveted iron mask, bare arms bolted through, the red banner on his back. Every letter panzermensch.zs names: A-D the walk (Spawn idles on A B), E F G the attack (he draws back with the shell forming in his fist, cocks it over his head, throws: the sweep's G), H the pain, at 8 drawn rotations; I-O the death at rotation 0. 71 lumps. No XDeath: Doom's bruisers do not gib (BESTIARY 8). The sweep's views are its pixels byte for byte (SDPMA1, SDPMA3, SDPMA5, SDPMG1).

EIGHT ROTATIONS, NOT MIRRORED PAIRS. The banner carries the D12 symbol, which a mirror reverses, and he throws with his right.

THE DEATH. Hit in the chest at I (the burst, the stain on the plate), his knees go at J, he falls back over his heels twisting (K), hits the floor (L) and lies on his back across the screen; head, arms (from the shoulder, then the forearm from the elbow) and legs are each let fall until they touch, and the pool grows under his chest and head (M-O). Measured on the corpse O: every part within 2 units of the floor (section 5), the body 21.86 units high at most unscaled (the test: under 26), the supports spread 60.6 units, the torso lying across the screen (1.0). Blood is I 2%, J 8%, K 11%, L 15%, M 22%, N 26%, O 33% of the death frames' pixels.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. He is the darkest thing in the mod, as BESTIARY 2.1 wants, so in Z10 at light 96 he is a dark shape with khaki arms and skull; the plate reads very dark olive, not neutral black. The walk's four frames differ mostly in the legs from the front (side views show the stride). The wind-up E hides the forming shell behind him from the front. The corpse is seen from 37 degrees, as the approved hound's. The death frames' blood spray is 1-2 px dots at 1x: spray in motion, noise on a still.

NOT VERIFIED. Outside the engine (D26): the light stand-in, the rotation order, the grAb offsets (rig_core's, as every approved enemy). The PM's engine pass is pending.
