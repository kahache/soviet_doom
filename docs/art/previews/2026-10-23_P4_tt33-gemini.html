6. Notes for the decision

  • Hands, skin, sleeve and pixel size now match the six Gemini weapons (section 3); the old TT-33 is the only weapon with Freedoom's flat grey-pink hands and a squarer, closer gun.
  • The new pistol is smaller and further away than the old one (the hand reaches forward, the arm comes in from the bottom right), as the other Gemini weapons are drawn.
  • Gemini drew a generic modern pistol (squared slide, no visible hammer or star grip), not a recognisable TT-33. At 26 px wide it reads as "a pistol"; a re-ask with a stricter TT-33 description is proposed to the PM.
  • Gemini's firing image has no recoil (the gun does not move): the kick is the pipeline's, the same 3 px as the other weapons. The old TT-33 kicked 11 px.
