8 · Honest verdict and what you decide

WHAT THIS IS. D65: the Schwer is cell D with the sweep's S2 treatment, the PM's call: black plate, 12 % larger, the spikes AND the exhaust stacks and side skirts, the mask's slit lit. Its own set of every frame the Panzermensch's states name, under the proposed sprite SDPS (section 8 for the name and the class change). 71 lumps.

EIGHT ROTATIONS, NOT MIRRORED PAIRS. The banner carries the D12 symbol, which a mirror reverses, and he throws with his right.

THE DEATH. He cannot lie on his back: the stacks would prop him off the floor (the Legion's first L0 fault). Hit at I, he folds forward (J), falls on his face (K, L), and lies prone across the screen with the stacks and the banner on top, the pool spreading from under his chest. Measured on the corpse O: every part within 2 units of the floor (section 5), the body 25.42 units high at most unscaled (the test: under 26), the supports spread 52.0 units, the torso lying across the screen (0.999). Blood is I 2%, J 2%, K 7%, L 9%, M 11%, N 12%, O 18% of the death frames' pixels.

WHAT DOES NOT, HONESTLY. He is the darkest thing in the mod, as BESTIARY 2.1 wants, so in Z10 at light 96 he is a dark shape with khaki arms and skull; the plate reads very dark olive, not neutral black. The walk's four frames differ mostly in the legs from the front (side views show the stride). The wind-up E hides the forming shell behind him from the front. The corpse is seen from 37 degrees, as the approved hound's. The death frames' blood spray is 1-2 px dots at 1x: spray in motion, noise on a still. The sprite is 12 % taller than the hitbox it inherits (section 8).

NOT VERIFIED. Outside the engine (D26): the light stand-in, the rotation order, the grAb offsets (rig_core's, as every approved enemy). The PM's engine pass is pending.

The name and the class change (for mod-engineer)

Lump name proposed: SDPS (SD + Panzermensch Schwer). It is free: no weapon or bestiary prefix of BESTIARY 2.3 or MOD-SPEC 6.3 starts with it, no lump of ours in assets/ or src/ does, and freedoom2.wad has none starting with SD. It needs a line in MOD-SPEC 6.3's bestiary table and in BESTIARY 2.3's prefix list.

What mod-engineer changes in SD_PanzermenschSchwer (src/zscript/monsters/panzermensch_schwer.zs): today the class has no States and inherits SD_Panzermensch's, so it draws whatever the base draws. It gets its own States block, a copy of the base's with SDPS for the sprite name and nothing else changed (Spawn SDPS AB 10 A_Look; See SDPS A 3 A_SD_PanzerStep, SDPS ABBCCDD 3 A_Chase; Melee/Missile SDPS EF 8, SDPS G 8 A_SD_MeleeOrMissile(...); Pain SDPS H 2, H 2 A_Pain; Death SDPS I-N 8 with A_Scream on J and A_NoBlocking on L, SDPS O -1; Raise SDPS O 8, NMLKJI 8). About 30 lines; A_SD_PanzerStep and the constants are inherited, so they resolve as they do today. The header comment's 'a Translation recolours it' goes.

Two consequences to weigh. (1) The sprite is 12 % taller (about 84 px) than the actor's Height 64, which the class inherits: a player shooting the top of the stacks hits nothing. Height 72 (64 x 1.12) would match the drawing and still clear the 72-unit arenas BESTIARY 8 asks for, but it changes where he fits, which is the level-designer's and the PM's call, not art's. Radius 24 x 1.12 = 27 likewise. (2) Its death differs on purpose (it falls on its face, the stacks forbid lying on its back): the Raise state reverses it as for the base.
