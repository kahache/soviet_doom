# Similar mods to "Soviet Doom" — state of the art (F0)

Date: 2026-10-09. Author: PM. Russian mirror: `docs/ru/product/`.
CEO question: does a mod with the premise "USSR invaded by Yankee and Nazi
aliens" already exist? Can we reuse assets with a suitable licence?

**Verdict: no mod with this premise exists.** Several projects have partial
Russian or Soviet themes, but none combines the USSR as protagonist, an alien
invasion and Yankees/Nazis as enemies, and none offers reusable assets with a
clear licence except those listed under "What we can use".

Limitation: Doomworld and ModDB block automated access, so the table comes
from search engines, DoomWiki and the ZDoom forum. The CEO checked the
blocked pages by hand (see below).

## What exists

| Mod | What it is | Overlap | Reusable assets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reds Over World** (WojtTheDoomer, 2024, GZDoom, Cacowards 2024 honourable mention) | 30-map Cold War TC in Command & Conquer style: WWIII USSR vs Allies; play as Spetsnaz, US Marine or European agent | high in Soviet settings (Moscow, subarctic base); no aliens | **No.** Mixes original assets with material extracted from other games (Far Cry 1 among them). No public copyright notice found (CEO checked) |
| **Bloody Steel** (BigMemka, 2014, Boom) | 10 maps, realistic post-Soviet industry with propaganda posters, TVs, soda machines | high in "Soviet paraphernalia" textures | **No.** Its idgames text file states that other authors may NOT use its contents as a base for modification or reuse (CEO verified). Inspiration only |
| **Russian Doom Time** (ModDB, alpha 0.1.3, April 2025, GZDoom) | TC about the Russian Civil War (1917–22), 3 maps | low: Russian, not Soviet; different era | no licence published |
| **Russian Overkill** (PillowBlaster, 2012–2025, GZDoom) | absurdly overpowered weapons with Russian flavour; Cacoward 2012 | low: weapons and humour only | no reuse licence |
| **Soviet Patch / Freedoom Soviet Patch** (ModDB) | Wolfenstein 3D soldiers recoloured red, shouting in Russian | none (CEO: "nothing to do with our game") | **No:** derived from id Software sprites |
| **CCCP 20**, **SJ Technoworld**, **Lilium**, **Whitemare 1/2** | single maps or community sets with Russian/Soviet touches | marginal | not applicable |
| GitHub repos named "Soviet DOOM" (3) | a two-day Zandronum WAD with "stupid soviet stuff" (no licence), a 2020 Unity game, a TypeScript project | none is a published mod | not applicable; name usable |

**`sci2` = "Scientist 2"** (author "Scientist", October 2005; read via the
gamers.org idgames mirror): 20 single-player maps in three episodes (Central
City, Siberia, Sacred City) plus 10 deathmatch maps, with new textures,
sprites, five weapon replacements (including an AK-47 and a flamethrower) and
music. The Siberia episode carries Lenin and Marx quotes; the author states no
political plot was intended. Permissions: "Authors may use the contents of
this file as a base for modification or reuse." So it is **reusable with
credit in principle**, but the text file does not document where its own
textures come from, so any piece we take must be checked for provenance
first. Filed as inspiration and a possible source for the Siberia set.

## What we can use (licence verified at the source)

- **Freedoom** (freedoom2.wad 0.13.0, January 2024): complete IWAD under a
  modified BSD licence. Base to play without owning Doom II; its textures and
  sprites can be reused and modified with attribution.
- **OTEX** (Ola Björling "ukiro", v1.1): high-quality texture pack. Author's
  terms: free redistribution with your levels, modification allowed, do not
  rename lumps, credit "Ola Björling (ukiro)", **no commercial use**.
- Soviet poster iconography: historical posters before 1930 are generally
  public domain, but it varies by author and country (NOT VERIFIED case by
  case). Safe path: **generate our own** (Cyrillic typography, constructivist
  composition, stylised portraits by script).

## Recommendation (accepted by the CEO)

1. No direct competitor: the premise is original.
2. Base IWAD = Freedoom (D2).
3. Textures: own scripts + OTEX (industry) + Freedoom (base sprites). Nothing
   from Reds Over World, Bloody Steel or id Software material.
4. Reds Over World and Bloody Steel may be **played** as reference, never
   copied.
