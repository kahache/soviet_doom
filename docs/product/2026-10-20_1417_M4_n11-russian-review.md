# N11: the one-time review of every Russian string the player sees (v1)

Date: 2026-10-20. Backlog N11, RELEASE.md row 6, D60 (done once, at the end).
Reviewer: `translator-ru`. Status: done; the PM checks the screens with
`language ru` (list at the end). Nothing was committed or staged; the engine
was not started.

## Scope and method

Read in full, string by string, against the English and the lore
(`docs/product/LORE.md`: USSR 1977, the Urals, Sergeant Ramon Vilanova, the
Fourth Reich and the Liberty Legion):

- the `[ru]` half of `src/lumps/LANGUAGE`: **162 ids** (159 are prose; three,
  `SD_PAUSE_SIGN`, `SD_SBAR_IMAGE`, `SD_SBAR_ARMS_IMAGE`, are picture names,
  checked only for existing);
- the Russian text in `tools/release.py`: the heading of the Russian credits,
  the six AI-disclosure paragraphs, the eight "made with" fragments and the
  Russian half of `README_TEMPLATE` (about 70 lines);
- the Russian half of `LICENSE.md` (about 20 paragraphs);
- the labels baked into `assets/graphics/statusbar/SDSBRU.png` and
  `SDARRU.png` (ПАТРОНЫ, ЗДОРОВЬЕ, ОРУЖИЕ, БРОНЯ): **correct**, not touched.

Checks run on every string: grammar, spelling, ё (scripted scan for 25 common
words that lose it; one hit, a false positive), typography («ёлочки», em
dash with spaces, decimal comma), idiom, one term per thing, 1977 register,
and fit. Fit was measured, not guessed: the 40-character credits rule and the
quit-line limit (tests), the 300 px finale width (`tests/test_campaign.py`),
the 78-character README line, and **glyph coverage**: a script confirmed every
character of every `[ru]` string (upper-cased, because `defsmallfont` has no
lowercase) exists in `assets/fonts/defsmallfont/` (А-Я, Ё, « », ×, №, the
en and em dash, ASCII). No character is missing.

Result: **17 `[ru]` strings changed**, 11 text blocks in `tools/release.py`
(README template, AI paragraphs, one heading), the Russian half of
`LICENSE.md` (reworded in about a dozen places), and one test expectation.
The remaining 145 `[ru]` strings were read and left alone. No `[enu]` string
and no id was touched; EN/RU id parity holds (`test_language.py`).

## What I could not settle (for the CEO or PM)

1. **Obituaries and declension.** `%o` and `%k` are player names and cannot be
   declined, so every line puts the killer last and undeclined («ледоруб %k»,
   «под залп %k», «от %k»). With Latin or Cyrillic nicknames this reads as
   acceptable gamer Russian, not as correct Russian, and no wording fixes it
   within the engine. The victim is also always masculine («застрелен»,
   «сгорел»). Obituaries print in multiplayer only, which v1 does not
   advertise; I left them.
2. **«Рейх» case in the other documents.** I followed the role spec («рейх»).
   `docs/ru/` (the mirror, out of scope until P3) mixes «Рейх» and «рейх». The
   mirror will need one pass when it is synced.
3. **Doughboy = «Каска» and the armour pickup «Стальная каска».** Different
   screens, the same word. If the CEO wants them told apart, the pickup could
   be «Стальной шлем».
4. **«Штурмовик-гибрид».** Correct for the SA stormtrooper, but a Russian
   player also hears the Il-2 ground-attack aircraft. Left; a rename is the
   CEO's (it is a name, D35).
5. **`SD_QUIT_04` «До парада три недели»** and the lore: the lore says 1977,
   the 60th anniversary of October; if the game is set in November, the
   parade is days away, not weeks. English says the same, so I did not change
   it; the CEO may want "три дня".
6. **`SD_CRED_MUSIC_9` has no final full stop** (40-character limit).
7. **`SD_MAP02_EXIT_TEXT`, «Колея во дворе слишком широка».** «Колея» is the
   right word for rail gauge but can also be read as a rut; kept because every
   alternative needed a longer line and the 300 px test is already tight on
   that screen. It is a judgement call, flagged.
8. **`tools/art/gen_items.py`** still carries the old backpack line («Ранец
   десантника…») as a drafted option record for the preview. It is not an
   in-game string and no test compares it with LANGUAGE; I did not touch it
   (not mine).
9. The menu verbs «ЗАГРУЗИТЬ» and «СОХРАНИТЬ» are the short forms of
   «ЗАГРУЗИТЬ ИГРУ» / «СОХРАНИТЬ ИГРУ»; kept because the menu font is wide and
   the CEO approved this menu (D31). The PM's screen will show whether the
   long forms would fit.
