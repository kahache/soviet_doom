# PM proposals for the next versions (2026-10-23)

Requested by the CEO after his playtest of episodes 2 and 3 (D119). Ordered by
value to the player against cost. Each carries an estimate in k tokens
(D69, ±50 %). None is decided: the CEO chooses.

## A. Content

| # | Proposal | Why | k tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Episode 4** (MAP10-12), after deciding whether we go to the invaders' world through the portal or to a new invasion somewhere else in the USSR | The ending already says «CONTINUARÀ»; it is the biggest content jump | 900 |
| A2 | **Events on picking up keys and items** (V3, ICEBOX): doors that open, walls that drop, ambushes | The CEO asked for it; adds surprise to the existing maps without redoing them | 150 (9 maps) |
| A3 | **Map set pieces**: a train that pulls out in MAP03, a dam that bursts in MAP05, lifts and floors that sink with monsters | Memorable moments, classic Doom style | 250 |
| A4 | **Hand grenade** (RGD-5) as a throwable weapon with its own ammo | Gives another option against the shotgun and is very Soviet | 120 |
| A5 | **Hand Katyusha** in a secret (MAP07 question pending) | A reward weapon for exploring | 80 |

and publication (**D2/D3**).
